### R06 — Robbins et al., Goal-directed learning in cortical organoids, Cell Reports 2026

- **Source:** [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41720084/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41720084/)
- **Scope:** Adaptive feedback learning on task, but no retained improvement after tested 45-minute rest.

### R07 — Cai et al., Brain organoid reservoir computing for artificial intelligence, Nature Electronics 2023

- **Source:** [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-023-01069-w](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-023-01069-w)
- **Scope:** A bounded hybrid neural-computing precedent, not a full semantic mind.

### R08 — Williamson et al., Learning-associated astrocyte ensembles regulate memory recall, Nature 2025

- **Source:** [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08170-w](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08170-w)
- **Scope:** Mouse in-vivo astrocyte recall evidence; does not establish organoid memory storage.

### R09 — Dewa et al., The astrocytic ensemble acts as a multiday trace to stabilize memory, Nature 2025

- **Source:** [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09619-2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09619-2)
- **Scope:** Mouse slow-state stabilization candidate; nonportable to organoid without tests.

### R10 — Taga et al., Human spinal astrocyte influence on motor neuron maturation, Stem Cells Translational Medicine 2019

- **Source:** [https://doi.org/10.1002/sctm.19-0147](https://doi.org/10.1002/sctm.19-0147)
- **Scope:** Spinal regional co-culture maturation, not general learning.

### R11 — Osaki et al., Reciprocally connected cerebral organoids, Nature Communications 2024

- **Source:** [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-46787-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-46787-7)

### R12 — Tsokas et al., KIBRA/PKMzeta memory maintenance, Science Advances 2024

- **Source:** [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11204205/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11204205/)
