# How to read the inventory

Each record is a scientific or engineering candidate, not a combined ingredient list. SOURCE-REFERENCE means appears in a named platform/culture reference; ASSAY means measure rather than add; PRODUCTION-ONLY and RESEARCH-ONLY are not living maintenance prescriptions. Chemical identity, mechanism, dose, lifetime, lot, and targeted recipients are independent fields. \[R01, R13, R16\]

# Four distinct teaching / support channels

| **Channel**                    | **ChristEye role**                                    | **Ordinary carrier**                                  | **Minimum proof**                                                               |
|--------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Nutrient support               | Milk / Bread when explicitly a metabolic support role | Defined medium, ions, glucose, amino acids, lipids    | Identity, local adequacy, measured in/out, viable release.                      |
| Information provision          | Bread / Meat as curricula                             | Document chunks, examples, code, lessons, assessments | Correctness, prerequisites, transfer, replay consent.                           |
| Learning feedback              | Honey as outcome-contingent information               | Versioned electrical/host events, not nutrients       | Independent contingency and matched controls.                                   |
| Candidate molecular modulation | Word / receiving state / Honey subrole                | Specified receptor-interacting molecule or peptide    | Specificity, target engagement, effect/clearance, independent biosafety review. |

Direct sugar/starch/protein meals or a peptide pill are not a justified mechanism for programming semantic knowledge into a culture. The first research reference uses defined soluble media and supplements; any new formulation must be independently qualified. Daily monitoring is separate from change frequency. \[R01, R13, R15\]

# Research ladder for a molecular Word, without conflating with a Crystal

1.  Name the exact molecular entity, form, compartment and receiving receptor; verify it exists in this preparation.

2.  Show that changing signal identity or order changes a measured receiving-state endpoint beyond generic activity.

3.  Demonstrate task-specific retained function after teaching ends, using independently controlled interface/readout.

4.  Identify a bounded physical carrier and show material state predicts and contributes to retained content.

5.  Declare the reader contract, compare incompatible reader and digital-only matched controls.

6.  Only then propose portability, cloning or molecular transfer; do not treat reconstruction after retraining as exact engram transfer.

# The key candidate physical scale

Potential internal scaffolds (PSD-95, SynGAP, Shank, Homer), surrounding PNN/ECM constraints, slower astrocytic state, and KIBRA–PKMzeta maintenance are separate hypotheses. A dense or gel-like assembly is not a crystallized memory by itself. The revised ChristEye equation audit rejects the old claim that a logistic maintenance equation automatically yields bistability. \[R12, R15-R16\]
