The project includes many prior designs. They often illustrate candidate mechanisms or programming abstractions; their completeness is not evidence that a physical Crystal, peptide upgrade or autonomously grown organoid exists. ## Evidence classification

F: independently cited finding in the named preparation. X: interpretation across work. H: testable hypothesis. D: design decision. A: analogy. Keep physical Root and Crystal status separate from digital archive and replay competence. A successful simulator trial cannot validate tissue learning. Cell production requires source release and institutional approval; remote telemetry does not satisfy any laboratory SOP.

## Publication and privacy

The original Six Stars stream log is a raw application transcript containing rich conversation and debug material. It is stored only in the private archive within the local full ZIP. Public Pages receives curated summaries and explicit timestamps where supported; claims of exact word-for-word ChatGPT export are forbidden. A contributor must inspect personal data, licenses and IP before committing any archive. This approach preserves the project record while avoiding inadvertent disclosure.
