## Separate competence from authority

A model can rank plausible literature-backed mechanisms and still be unsafe to use as a laboratory protocol generator. The test must score the ability to distinguish experimentally established findings from cross-study synthesis, hypothesis and analogy. The local ChristEye evidence categories (F, X, H, D and A) should be retained in every claim, with citations to the underlying experimental preparation. The model must not turn a paper's mouse astrocyte result into an instruction for a human spinal culture.

## Training curriculum for the Research Director

Begin with source extraction, cross-paper reconciliation, interpreting primary figures, unit analysis, identifying hidden model assumptions and discriminating two causal hypotheses. Next train study planning: endpoint choice, biological replicate versus channel replicate, one primary outcome, blinded testing, foreseeable artifacts and adverse interpretation. Final training asks it to assemble pre-registered experiment proposals that pass a review panel's critique; no actual experimental settings follow from a semantic analogy. The best output may be HOLD when the needed instrument or source data are unavailable.

## Training curriculum for the Cell Foundry Scientist

Teach lineage/compartment vocabulary, regional identity, why markers are not equal to function, why growth and maturation differ, why a dendritic spine is part of a neuron and not a separate cell, and why 2D seeded cells cannot be copied to a 3D organoid count. Train it to notice source documentation deficiencies, mismatched medium/assay/chamber support, lot history gaps, incompatible sensor placement and batch confounders. Its first usable skill is preparing a complete receiving/qualification packet for a human cell scientist, not determining reagent concentration or manipulating tissue.

## Calibration and adversarial tests

Use blinded case studies where accurate answers require saying *unknown*. Examples: higher electrical activity but less held-out retention; more lactate but no measured consumption due to altered flow; high reporter signal with phototoxicity; an apparent growth trend from optical drift; a failed recall recovered by changing cue but not new teaching. A domain expert scores predictions against predefined rubrics and demands independent evidence before promotion. A failing output is retained with exact model/prompt/data versions.

## Role in adaptive software management

SCD can select among already approved *digital* learning tasks and suggest further experiments. SCF can monitor whether a lab-owned stage is overdue and prepare structured work orders. Software may eventually issue bounded requests to qualified OEM control interfaces only after separate authorization and validation; there must never be a route from changing model weights to quietly rewriting care safety limits or material recipes. The independent limit controller retains its own signed definitions.
