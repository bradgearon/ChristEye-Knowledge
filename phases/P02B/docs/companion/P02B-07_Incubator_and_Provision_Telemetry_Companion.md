## Measurement interpretation

Incubator air temperature and CO2 are environmental measures; they do not directly reveal medium at the cell surface, local pH, viability or task information. The same applies to reservoir-sampled lactate or glucose. When a service precedes a recall improvement, plausible confounders include elapsed time, sensor drift, medium carryover and altered excitability. A causal support claim needs matched schedules and independent outcome testing.
