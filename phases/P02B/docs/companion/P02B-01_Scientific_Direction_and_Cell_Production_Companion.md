## Science director perspective

The team must state whether it is evaluating a **viable sample, an electrical response, adaptive performance, retained performance, joined-module contribution, or an independently learned recipient**. These are nested but nonidentical hypotheses. Cell identity and function are different evidence; reporter expression is not equivalent to functional maturity. A producer may release a well-characterized culture while the scientist still has no evidence that it learned.

## Production scientist perspective

Maintain explicit distinction among *source expansion*, *lineage differentiation*, *maturation*, *receiving into an instrument*, and *task training*. Each transition has a separate release and stability record. Do not derive a 3D organoid starting count from 2D planar seeded-area numbers. Preserve lineage subtypes and source-related variation as covariates rather than pooling all preparations under “Spirit” or “Soil.”

## Replication and study design

The independently prepared culture/organoid is generally the biological statistical unit, not electrodes, frames or repeated tasks. Report the precise unit used for randomization, batch/nested records, attrition, blinded scoring and preregistered contrasts. Keep an independent confirmation set. A claimed retained Root requires performance on a delayed and answer-free task with a frozen decoder. If the same user demonstration supplies task labels, conditioning examples and confirmation answers, the evidence is leaked.

## Device-oriented governance

For every source or run, the research database stores preparation_id, source_line_id, source_lot, model_version, operator, SOP_ref, instrument_config_hash, assay_quality, observations, deviations, exit_gate, and claim_scope. Null is permitted when not yet known, but null blocks the relevant gate. No software system fabricates a biological parameter from an analogy.

## Policy on experimental complexity

More immune-like cells, brainstem-like circuits, or directed progenitors are each new preparation versions requiring scientific purpose and review; “anti-disease Spirit” is initially a surveillance/integrity claim, not a therapeutic entity. As models become more complex, revisit relevant organoid ethical oversight and donor data/privacy rather than assuming the original approval remains sufficient.

This companion is a scoped scientific interpretation. Distinguish instrument measurements, delayed task evidence and mechanism attribution. Model corrections in Formal Models and Reconciliation (source S-AUDIT) take precedence over historic scalar Crystal claims.
