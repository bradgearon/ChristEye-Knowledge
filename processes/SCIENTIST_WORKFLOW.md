# Anaiero scientific director's full cross-module workflow — 2026-11-15

**Role:** scientist-facing process packet and signed handoff; **scope:** experimental design and qualification, not a stand-alone cell manufacturing or peptide administration SOP. The [24 current module designs](../specifications/v3/README.md) and [10 living-science record schemas](../schemas/biology/README.md) reference this workflow.

## The system to build

- The **living Soil** is an identified organoid, including neuronal/glial cells, axons, dendrites, local matrix and developmental state.
- The **spinal Root** is a candidate physical system with tested regional identity; the **functional Root** is a scored retained learned relation. Data connects them only with evidence.
- The **Crest** is production lineage and source quality management; it does not encode a new semantic fact into each baby cell.
- **SustiBus** records external qualified care and physiology independently of tasks; **ChemiBus** records assay-qualified chemical observations; **WindBus** transports digital instructions/measurements; **BioBridge** is the qualified instrumentation interface.
- A **digital Crystal** is a versioned software teaching package. A **physical Crystal** is an unverified potential memory-bearing biological carrier with a distinct proof requirement.

## Developmental transition gate — detailed review form

For every proposed change, the scientist must answer:

1. **What is changing?** Cell division, source identity, axon extension, synapse remodeling, receptor state, matrix, metabolism, digital codebook, decoder or actual trained behavior?
2. **What mechanism is hypothesized?** Ordinary biological target, molecular form and compartment with original literature. A pathway name does not specify a reagent dose.
3. **Where will it be observed?** Cell/region/spinal Root/Soil, electrode field, RNA/protein sample and its actual spatial and clock limitations.
4. **What is known before intervention?** Viability, qualified medium, source batch, developmental state, learned old Roots, axon map, sensor calibration and logged task conditions.
5. **Which alternative explanation might fool us?** Shared input, learning-label leakage, readout improvement, more cells without useful connections, instrument drift, metabolism changes, or simple exposure.
6. **What can be preserved?** Existing skills, lineage identity, functional axon routes, stable source support and ability to independently assess the subject after the intervention.
7. **Who authorizes the next stage?** PI, cell foundry, instrumentation and ethical/biosafety review with actual SOP and signed study plan.
8. **What disconfirms the theory?** Predeclared negative and inconclusive outcomes, with the number of independent biological preparations and uncertainty calculation.
9. **How is the system retired safely?** Do not assume a digital rollback restores previous biological matter.
10. **What is publishable?** The actual measured scoped result with source/lot and methodology, not an unsupported general mind, autonomous drug, or physical memory-clone claim.

## Cross-system handoff artifacts

- **A. Source file:** supplier/source, regional identity, production lot, consent and qualified procedure version.
- **B. Structural file:** tissue geometry, observed regions, cell type confidence, axonal tracks, candidates and reference images.
- **C. Chemistry/care file:** actual medium identity and independent service receipts, sample-based glucose/lactate readings, sensor validity and predeclared support adequacy.
- **D. Electrical file:** OEM channel/electrode mapping, physical cue receipt, timing anchors, artifact masks and frozen reader.
- **E. Teaching file:** context and ordered stimuli, feedback information budget, independent sealed evaluator and controls.
- **F. Root file:** per-class performance, delayed score, protected task changes and candidate physical substrate evidence.
- **G. Research material file:** molecule and molecular-form registry, receptor context, observed RNA/protein or physical carrier result, interpretation limits.
- **H. Approval file:** human signoffs, change proposal and no-actuation digital controls.
- **I. Archive:** immutable data hashes, negative results, source references, package version and WDS licensing record.

## Exact decision branches

If a **delayed task declines**: first exclude broken data retrieval/codebook, changed reader, missing input, tissue health and ordinary maturation. If alternate cues restore performance without teaching, one access-loss hypothesis is supported but precise memory location remains unknown. If a **molecular state changes**: test whether it predicts specific Root performance beyond a health/age-matched baseline. If an **axon grows**: require functional recipient influence and later task benefit rather than assigning it as a new Root by morphology alone.

## Cell IDs from Crests

The proposed first implementation uses **external UUIDs linked to actual observed lineage**. A CloneLineageID is shared among descendants, and even an introduced genetic lineage barcode is not automatically unique per baby. A laboratory proposal for any genomic insertion needs independent ethics, collision/dropout, readout specificity, perturbation and inheritance assessments before manufacturing design.
