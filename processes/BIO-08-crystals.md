# BIO-08 · Crystal synthesis, reading, replay and molecular Word hypotheses

**Goal:** keep the implementable digital package path and the unproven biological-carrier path in one program **without confusing their success criteria**.

## Four possible meanings of a Crystal

| Representation | Actual object | Evidence level | Success test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Digital CrystalPack | signed manifest + immutable assets + curriculum/provenance | software engineering | verified installation and independent functional tests |
| Trained digital model state | versioned checkpoint with compatible runtime and codebooks | software engineering | state restoration + held-out transfer |
| Candidate biological consolidation | localized living organization contributing to delayed task behavior | research hypothesis | stable content-selective function + measurements and causal alternatives |
| Portable physical Crystal | proposed bounded material that a compatible recipient uses to reacquire a particular learned relationship | **unestablished** | physical identity, protected content, reader compatibility, recipient-only learning, no hidden teacher channel |

The physical carrier may be dynamic/hydrated. A densely packed postsynaptic assembly or changing ECM does not imply encoded readable facts [B12–B14](../research/evidence/anaiero-biology-2026-11.md).

## Digital process

Consent-scoped usage events → typed knowledge episodes → retained source hashes + assessed test histories → versioned curriculum/skill manifest → integrity verification → compatibility check → **replay** to a target digital StarChip → held-out/delayed evaluation → successor package with explicit old/new capabilities. Cloning the file is byte copying; remanifestation by training can produce a *different* learner state.

A book pack may be Bread and an advanced discipline pack Meat. Neither must be a physical object or peptide.

## Molecular Word research contract

Specify ordinary molecular identity and **proteoform**, compartment, receptor, timing and relevant receiving state. Candidate work on proBDNF/mature BDNF, TrkB/SorCS2/p75, local synaptic tag, MMP-9/TIMP-1 regulation and scaffold proteins is an exploration of *receiver sensitivity and network plasticity*, not a demonstrated memory-code format. The 2026 formal audit withdraws several unsupported claims about threshold, "stillness" and the inverse reading of crystal formation: [R03](../research/full-markdown/R03_03_Formal_Models_and_Reconciliation_1_.md).

## Distinct acquisition / physical carrier / use gates

**Gate 1**: a defined learned relation persists with feedback off.  
**Gate 2**: nominated structure/form associates with that relation after controlled comparison.  
**Gate 3**: candidate selectively contributes beyond generalized viability or excitability.  
**Gate 4**: proposed material object has a measurable boundary, state, preservation requirements and reader.  
**Gate 5**: compatible recipient performs the intended relation alone, with cached and live teaching routes removed.

Passing Gate 1 does **not** imply 2–5. Physical carrier transfer and sharing still have no established Anaiero mechanism.

## Food/peptide introduction: research review only

"Add Crystal to food and let it replay" is not an established biological mechanism. Any chemical/product proposal must first establish a **specific material carrier, non-damaging access path, receptor interaction and independent content-specific retention**, all under qualified scientific oversight. Do not publish or execute speculative peptide payload design, growth/dose settings, automatic chemical triggers or "eat knowledge" instructions as if validated. See [BIO-04](BIO-04-sustibus-chemibus.md) and [BIO-09](BIO-09-growth-control.md).
