## Common vocabulary and interpretation

**Soil** = the proposed living organoid as a whole, including cells, matrix, neural/axonal structures and history. The surrounding **Sea** is the exchange environment; **Water** is transport and access *in a declared context*.

**Root tissue** = the spinal-derived neural structures the Anaiero design proposes to use as foundation/coordination material. **Root learning** = a tested durable functional relation; do not claim they are co-located without evidence. **Crest** = the production/service identity role for source lineages, **not** automatically a newly invented cellular phenotype.

**Axon** is the signal-transmitting neurite of a neuron, not another cell. Its existence, directed connectivity and relation to learning require distinct microscopy and function tests.

**Sustibus** = physical support status and source-qualified care records. **Chemibus** = chemical analyte measurement and candidate intervention proposals, never a substitute for a tissue-care authorization. **WindBus** = typed digital information traffic. **BioBridge** = separately qualified instrument adapter; it must not silently couple unrelated source networks.

## Card required in each design: Provision & peptide change

The card must include trigger, observed need, evidence required, human PI and cell-production approval, predeclared target and protected function, comparator, adverse-state monitoring, rollback/stop plan and whether the route is digital or living. **No adaptive learner may decide to change formulation, introduce peptides or trigger uncontrolled cell division.**

## Approved versus unresolved

An explicitly recorded null is better than an invented density, feeding interval, concentration, spinal lineage recipe, growth-factor schedule, genetic barcode method or electrical stimulation profile. Vendor and laboratory qualifications determine all such parameters for each actual preparation.
