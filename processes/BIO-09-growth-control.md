# BIO-09 · Growth, refresh, maintenance and intervention governance

**Objective:** decide whether a desired learning capability warrants *any* structural expansion rather than assuming more cells, more axons, more peptides or stronger stimulation means better Understanding.

## Four independent meanings of growth

| Change | Real substrate | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| digital capacity | more storage/index/model tools/specialist modules | replay correctness, performance, cost and regression |
| new cell production | progenitor division and a qualified lineage | imaged birth/lineage/source, viability and phenotype |
| anatomical remodeling | existing neurites, axonal trajectories, synapses, glial coverage, ECM | registered structural comparison with imaging validity |
| learned functional capability | newly acquired and retained relation after teaching | independent held-out/delayed tests, matched controls |

New cells cannot be ordered from a mature neuron culture by assuming spontaneous "birth." Axons and dendritic spines are **parts** of neurons; morphological growth is not clonal multiplication. Clone a digital Crystal by copying verified assets; biological descendants share lineage, not necessarily learned state.

## Stage-gated decision procedure

1. **Observe** — collect a task-specific deficit or measured structural/health change with source and sensor validity.
2. **Diagnose** — compare acquisition, retrieval, bad codebook/decoder, external source staleness, data routing, maturation and actual support inadequacy.
3. **Repair digital path first** — attempt authorized index rebuild, software retrieval, curriculum retest and frozen-reader evaluation.
4. **Decide whether new biological capacity is even a meaningful hypothesis.** Require a biological deficit not explained by earlier causes; independent PI declares a specific target and measured mechanism.
5. **Stage a separate research proposal** — qualified cell source, tissue identity, why additional cell type or axon process is needed, independent approved SOP, endpoint, control and protected old skills.
6. **Observe after approved laboratory activity** — compare measured tissue/axon/chemical changes and delayed functional effects; report unfavorable and unresolved cases.
7. **Promote to next stage only on independent evidence**, not by self-assessment or model confidence.

## Model for digital prioritization

For declared competencies U_j and allowed digital actions a, use an explicitly documented tradeoff of expected benefit, computational cost and compatibility. Each protected skill has a distinct noninferiority rule with prechosen margin, uncertainty and test context. The independent protection controller vetoes physical actions. An uncontrolled biological action cannot be part of the optimization set.

## Tissue-development and molecular candidate classes

Regional source markers, axon/dendritic morphology, selected synaptic scaffolds, astrocytic and ECM regulatory states and trophic-receptor activity can be **measured** as a biological study. BDNF/TrkB and ECM regulators have context-dependent effects in literature [B14](../research/evidence/anaiero-biology-2026-11.md); none supplies a universal growth schedule for an Anaiero device.
