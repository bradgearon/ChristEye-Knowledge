# BIO-03 · Soil: organoid development, regions and observed maturity

**Owner:** qualified organoid developmental scientist and cell foundry. **Status:** model-specific qualification design, not a live organoid-production protocol. **Related:** [Crest](BIO-01-crest-lineage.md), [spinal Roots / axons](BIO-02-axon-spinal-root.md), [measurement](BIO-05-measurements.md), [growth](BIO-09-growth-control.md).

## Scope of the Soil

**Soil is the entire living organoid**, not culture medium or extracellular matrix alone. It contains declared neuronal and glial populations, their soma and projections, local ECM, developmental history and measured contact with the surrounding **Sea**. A planar spinal neuron–astrocyte study qualifies instrument behavior but does not certify a 3D organoid: source, geometry, oxygen/solute distribution, tissue heterogeneity and deep observation are distinct preparation variables. See [B01](../research/evidence/anaiero-biology-2026-11.md), [B08](../research/evidence/anaiero-biology-2026-11.md), [B18](../research/evidence/anaiero-biology-2026-11.md).

## Stage and exit matrix

| State | Necessary measured result | Must not infer |
|---|---|---|
| SOURCE_IDENTIFIED | source lot, permissions, lineage/regional objective and responsible qualified laboratory | differentiated cell competence |
| STRUCTURE_OBSERVED | tissue boundary, region map, size, coverage, viability and uncertainty | uniform internal organization |
| LINEAGE_CHARACTERIZED | sampled neuronal/glial subtype identity, spatial distribution and batch consistency | every cell's fate |
| AXON_AND_MESH_OBSERVED | selected neurites, axon pathways, candidate synapses, glial contact | functionally communicating synapses |
| ELECTRICAL_BASELINE | qualified acquisition, stimulus artifact control and reproducible state observations | trained Knowledge |
| EDUCATION_ELIGIBLE | frozen task encoding, delivery receipts, evaluator separation, protected care, lab signoff | ability to learn any arbitrary instruction |
| ROOT_CANDIDATE | delayed, content-selective task performance with matched controls | a located engram or physical Crystal |

**Measurement plan:** nuclear and cell-membrane spatial fields, selected actin/tubulin dynamics, neuron/astrocyte/OPC regional markers, candidate axon tracks, dendritic arbor/spines, electrical bursts and task-selective features, metabolites, sample location, instrument depth/coverage and imaging perturbation. Longitudinal microscopy provides precedent but must account for illumination/handling artifacts [B06](../research/evidence/anaiero-biology-2026-11.md).

## Material inventory by class (observation, not recipe)

**Identity:** SOX2 and progenitor/regional markers appropriate to source, neuronal TUBB3/MAP2/RBFOX3, candidate spinal MNX1/ISL1/CHAT, astrocytic SOX9/ALDH1L1/GFAP, optional later OLIG2/SOX10 and microglial phenotype when separately sourced.  
**Projection/synapse:** axonal neurofilament/MAPT-associated signals, growth-linked GAP43 as assay candidate, dendritic MAP2, synaptophysin/SYN1, DLG4/SHANK/HOMER, receptors and selected local calcium/activity.  
**Support and remodeling:** glucose/lactate/oxygen/pH/osmolality, selected MCT transport, ECM components, MMP-9/TIMP-1 as *local activity hypotheses*, not a universal Soil gate.

## Production and maturation control architecture

Crest creates a source request; the human cell lead owns a validated SOP; the observer records characterized tissue state. The software **may propose** comparing different qualified preparation versions, not command division, differentiation, medium changes or matrix opening. No universal developmental day or cell count is specified for 3D Soil. A visible larger organoid is not more competent without independent task evaluation. Keep parent/descendant lineage metadata and compartment-specific cell observations.

## Failure states

When assay/region coverage is insufficient: label UNKNOWN_COVERAGE, not "new Root". When support/viability is suspect: pause task experimentation while sustaining separately qualified care. When source and tissue disagree: quarantine interpretation pending human review. A neural burst or structural densification never automatically promotes a physical Crystal claim.

## Required proposal card: medium, food or peptide

Trigger, preparation/batch, diagnosed need, qualified medium lot, specific candidate mechanism/target, expected benefit, matched comparator, axon and lineage preservation, old Root noninferiority, independent care approval, signed research plan, stop rule, post-study measurements. **No software actuation or unvalidated dosing.**
