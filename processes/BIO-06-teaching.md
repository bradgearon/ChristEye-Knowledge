# BIO-06 · Spirit education, acquisition, delayed Roots and stabilization

**Scientific scope:** computer-assisted experimental **assessment** of selected learned relations. The same message format can run digitally and with a separately qualified living interface; shared format does not establish a common biological learning mechanism.

Spirits are named functional/cellular roles, not scientifically validated autonomous minds. A network's unexpected output is not proof of its independent intentions.

## Learning protocol as a state machine

1. **Define one relation**: C0/C1 context + ordered A/B stimuli + GO → X/Y/HOLD. Invalid inputs are UNRESOLVED, not a learnable output. The original program's constant HOLD baseline must be scored separately.
2. **Characterize reception**: verify the living substrate distinguishes the input patterns under a *frozen* readout and qualified delivered-event receipts. A waveform is not automatically a semantic Word.
3. **Train within a declared comparison**: record contingent feedback separately from essential Milk and chemical support. Use noncontingent/yoked and time-matched controls; match physical exposure only within an independently qualified instrument specification.
4. **Intervening period**: any rest, continued spontaneous activity or replay remains measured rather than assumed to induce sedimentation or "sleep"; do not withdraw vital care.
5. **Delayed retention**: feedback and teacher route off, codebook/decoder frozen, declared delay and uncertainty, prior skills explicitly protected. Report absolute scores and a chance-corrected ratio only when its denominator is valid.
6. **Transfer and interference**: withheld contexts, new tasks and old-task degradation measured separately.
7. **Joined use and B-only acquisition**: host-only, partner-only, joined and information-matched control cases; evaluate B alone only after cached teacher outputs and adaptive shared state are cleared.

The 2026 cortical-organoid learning demonstration reported limited persistence in its tested task; it does not validate broad Anaiero "Winds" [B15](../research/evidence/anaiero-biology-2026-11.md).

## Molecular and structural *candidate roles*

Ordinary synaptic electrophysiology implicates excitatory and inhibitory reception, presynaptic release machinery, dendritic receptor/scaffold organization, activity-dependent gene/protein regulation and neuron–astrocyte metabolic support. Candidate annotations for a study include AMPA/NMDA/GABA/glycine receptors, SYN1/synaptophysin, PSD-95/SynGAP, CaMKII, ARC, BDNF/TrkB and KIBRA/PKMζ—**not a prescriptive set of inputs to teach concepts**. [B07](../research/evidence/anaiero-biology-2026-11.md), [B11–B14](../research/evidence/anaiero-biology-2026-11.md).

## The Lotus decision model

Let U be an outcome vector for retention, transfer, flexibility and calibration, with each metric's own units. Optimize an explicitly weighted *digital curriculum* score subject to hard support and protected Root constraints. Do not infer a physical growth route from the optimizer, and never let sealed labels enter the adaptive learner before trial closure.

## Food/peptide and growth decision card

A poor score alone can initiate **more evaluation**, not medium alteration. Record task failure and physiological observations independently; only an approved mechanistic hypothesis with target/assay and physiological comparators may enter laboratory review. The card must list nonlearning support needs, current preparation/SOP, candidate molecule or pathway, alternative causes, protected old skills, axon/tissue integrity, authority and stopping rules. No unvalidated peptide sequence, dose or electrical regimen is emitted.
