# 1 Purpose and boundary

An independent constraint authority refuses unsafe, unauthorized or scientifically unqualified actions; biological support remains separate from task rewards and learned policy proposals.

# 2 Inputs

- Requested action, provenance, permissions, context and resource costs

- Signed policy table and current device safety state

- Relevant protected competencies and research status

# 3 Outputs / Winds

- PERMIT, DENY, HOLD or REVIEW_REQUIRED with reason and policy ID

- Immutable incident receipt and affected route list

- Safe fallback recommendation that does not assert recovery

# 4 Required behavior

- Hard limits are not traded away for higher task scores.

- Keep biological life-support control out of learning-module authority.

- Treat uncertain biological operations as manual institutional review.

- Separate intellectual property, content licensing and platform authorization.

# 5 Processing and activation

> **1.** Check authentication, asset signature and entitlement.
>
> **2.** Verify target-scoped rate, resource and data-use budgets.
>
> **3.** Evaluate protected capabilities and declared safety constraints.
>
> **4.** Log decision, execute only via permitted services, and audit after action.

# 6 Test and release gate

- An untrusted module cannot modify its own permissions.

- A biological module cannot directly change fluid composition or stimulation envelope.

- Safety remains functional when the generative AI model crashes.

# 7 Failure and protection

- Policy drift or model prompt injection altering apparent permissions.

- Confusing a candidate molecule with a qualified instruction.

# 8 Open research and engineering questions

- How will institutional approval artifacts be versioned?

# 9 Data contract and evidence trace

All calls use versioned request/response schemas. A WindEnvelope records source, recipient, trace_id, data type, delivered time, validity, provenance and uncertainty. Actual hardware commands, if any, require a separate approved permit. Performance claims cite evaluator runs with sealed test sets.
