**MASTER SYSTEM SPECIFICATION / GITHUB EDITION \| CE-M00 \| PROPOSAL**

ChristEye Genesis \| Modular computing, CrystalPacks, and information-bearing Winds

| **DESIGN THESIS** Specialized StarChips host independently qualified capabilities. CrystalPacks describe skills, books, model adapters, tools, curricula or experimental carriers. A typed WindBus integrates their outputs. The Genesis governor teaches, assesses, coordinates and protects the system. Digital modules are buildable now; biological knowledge packing is not established. |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

# 1 System goals

- Provide GPU-like, modular specialization while using conventional GPU/CPU software in the first release.

- Compose a high-depth Metroidvania experience on the fly without invalid progression gates, broken canon or frame stalls.

- Install smaller book, genre, code-feature and professional-discipline packs through verifiable activation jobs.

- Measure whether a capability is loaded, acquired, retained, generalized and causally useful; do not merge these claims.

- Retain a rigorous research interface for neural hardware, physical Crystal candidates and molecule-mediated signaling without claiming content-carrying peptide upgrades already exist.

# 5 The Crystal activation lifecycle

> **1.** DISCOVER — list versioned interfaces and asset contents; do not execute payloads.
>
> **2.** VERIFY — integrity, signatures, rights, target compatibility and approval.
>
> **3.** STAGE — shadow copy, dependency closure, resource budget and rollback point.
>
> **4.** DRY_RUN — synthetic traffic, schema checks and reference baselines.
>
> **5.** SHADOW — run candidate on held-out tasks without changing live output.
>
> **6.** CALIBRATE — allowed digital adaptation or controlled learning, with evaluation separation.
>
> **7.** ASSESS — delayed retention, transfer, protected-root regressions and independent baselines.
>
> **8.** RELEASE — enable qualified Winds with version pinning and audit receipts.
>
> **9.** MONITOR — detect drift, conflicts, degradations and evidence expiry.

Warm-up time is empirical, not intrinsic to the Crystal name. A book index may be ready immediately; a digital adapter may need caching or calibration; biological acquisition is unproven and requires separately defined experiments.

# 6 Flagship: real-time deep Metroidvania

The Experience Director writes a world plan, not every pixel simultaneously. The graph solver proves progression reachability, the room generator proves traversability, combat simulates telegraphs, narrative checks canon, art compiles engine-ready assets, and the adaptive soundtrack queues musical transitions. Generation is speculative ahead of the player and always asynchronous relative to the 60 FPS frame deadline (16.67 ms).

# 7 Educational and specialist use cases

- Music genre pack: instrumentation, form, motif grammar, original melodies, arrangements and separate MIDI/event lanes; optional AHD/ARD notation as a versioned theory plugin.

- Scientific theorist pack: evidence graph, candidate explanations, measurable variables, rival predictions and defeaters; generated theories are not treated as discoveries.

- Book microcrystal: edition-bounded library index, quotes/rights, reading path and citation-aware retrieval; optional independent comprehension curriculum.

- College discipline: a syllabus graph spanning foundations, advanced topics, integration and transfer assessments; competence is measured per outcome, not inferred from a named degree.

- Software upgrade: signed executable tool/adapter with sandbox, compatibility and tests; can be shipped as a digital pack without pretending proteins run code.

# 8 Evaluation and equations

Understanding is the vector U = (retention R, generalization G, flexibility F, calibration C). Report each component, its uncertainty and the task/decoder version. The curriculum planner uses candidate benefit with protected-root constraints:

| **PROPOSED DECISION OBJECTIVE** choose policy π to maximize E\[Σ w_k ΔU_k − λ·cost(π)\], subject to P(ΔU_protected,j ≥ −ε_j) ≥ 1 − α_j and independent Heart vetoes. This is an engineering objective, not a discovered neural law. |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

A chance-normalized retention score R(Δ) = (P_delayed − P_chance)/(P_immediate − P_chance) is only interpretable when the immediate advantage over chance is materially positive; it can exceed 1 and needs uncertainty intervals. The independent unit for biological comparisons is a preparation/cartridge, not an electrode or trial.

# 9 Research interfaces and falsifiability

- Living tissue precedents support bounded adaptive reservoir behavior and short-term closed-loop learning, not a complete general-purpose biological mind.

- The physical Crystal hypothesis requires a defined carrier, boundaries, content claim, reader, selective delayed use and controls for generic support and decoder effects.

- A peptide may modulate receptor/plasticity mechanisms; an experience-specific semantic update through a peptide or pill has not been demonstrated. No animal or human delivery instructions are part of this repository.

- Teacher-to-recipient transfer claims require recipient-only delayed tests after disconnect and clearing shared state, compared with software teaching and shuffled controls.
