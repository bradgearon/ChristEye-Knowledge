**CE-A09 \| COMPANION • 14 NOV 2026**

Main Living Network: Soil, Spirit, Heart and Tree — Technical Companion

Mechanisms, alternatives, evidence, metrics and engineering handoff

# 01 ChristEye role interpretation

Soil: living organoid whole; Spirits: typed participants; Heart: response formation; Tree: learned relations; Wind: measured output.

Assignments are scoped engineering roles, not a universal claim that a term is one molecule, cell type or brain region. Physical Soil denotes the living organoid as a whole; external medium and exchange are the Sea.

# 02 Scientific and computational standing

Organoid preparation choice, species, regional identity, cell composition, supplier instructions and institutional approval are controlled external dependencies, not generated prescriptions.

F = named published finding in its preparation; X = cross-study synthesis; H = untested hypothesis; D = design decision; A = analogy. An untested chain remains H even if its components are established separately.

# 03 Model and quantitative interpretation

## Candidate network dynamics

> x\[t+1\] = f(W\*x\[t\] + B\*c\[t\] + G\*a\[t\] + D\*z\[t\]).

Assumption / applicability: A proposed state-space form, not a fitted biological law. z is a measured environmental block, not the Soil itself.

Report independently: Macro recall, availability, channel validity, network drift, delayed retention, route contribution, calibration.

# 04 Data transformation and experiment logic

Input record: Qualified stimulus profile identifiers; task context; sensor observations; care status

Output record: Measured neural response; decoded Wind; trial receipt; Root evidence

Implementation decision rule: Host H presents only authorized task events and records delivered events; neural activity remains a physical observation; decoder outputs a class or UNKNOWN; evaluator E scores after response closure and controls feedback disclosure.

## Counterfactual checks

- Valid input/output responses beyond artifacts

- Learning beyond matched noncontingent controls

- A claimed biology-driven result survives frozen digital readout tests

# 05 Distinguish competing causes

- Organoid is not a validated full mind

- Spinal cells and cerebral organoids are different preparations

- Morphology does not establish task understanding

Attribution rule: correlation, high activity, successful decoding, structural density, software adaptation and biological retention are different observations. Keep source attribution and negative controls before upgrading the claim.

# 08 Research and safety boundary

RESEARCH-ONLY. No culture production, growth, stimulation, nutrient dosing, peptide synthesis, ingestion, pathogen modification or medical treatment protocol is authorized.

Qualified laboratories, suppliers and institutional oversight own cell identity, support formulation, living handling and any physical intervention. A physical Crystal or behavior-editing peptide remains hypothetical and has no authorized installation command.
