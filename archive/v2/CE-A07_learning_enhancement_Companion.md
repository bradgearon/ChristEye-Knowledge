**CE-A07 \| COMPANION • 14 NOV 2026**

Learning Enhancement and Curriculum Network — Technical Companion

Mechanisms, alternatives, evidence, metrics and engineering handoff

# 01 ChristEye role interpretation

Bread: prerequisites; Meat: advanced integrated knowledge; Honey: teaching feedback; Wind: lesson and expression.

Assignments are scoped engineering roles, not a universal claim that a term is one molecule, cell type or brain region. Physical Soil denotes the living organoid as a whole; external medium and exchange are the Sea.

# 02 Scientific and computational standing

Mapping the semantic curriculum to a biological input code is a research gap. A successful digital lesson is not a biological engram.

F = named published finding in its preparation; X = cross-study synthesis; H = untested hypothesis; D = design decision; A = analogy. An untested chain remains H even if its components are established separately.

# 03 Model and quantitative interpretation

## Curriculum impact

> Delta_U = U_adaptive - U_fixed on a preregistered held-out competency set.

Assumption / applicability: U is a vector; preserve separate dimensions instead of summing arbitrary units.

Report independently: Mastery on held-out competencies, sample efficiency, transfer beyond teaching examples, old-task noninferiority, curriculum benefit vs fixed schedule.

# 04 Data transformation and experiment logic

Input record: DeploymentPack; competency graph; source-rights record; learner performance

Output record: LessonPlan; training WindEvent; formative feedback; curriculum decision

Implementation decision rule: Rank lessons using expected learning benefit, novelty and uncertainty while enforcing a protected skill floor; randomize among comparably acceptable lessons during development to measure policy value.

## Counterfactual checks

- A module can advance only against a locked outcome

- Training examples do not contaminate confirmation

- Bread/Meat level is determined by prerequisites, not moral value

# 05 Distinguish competing causes

- A training signal may only displace state without learning

- Reward hacking produces superficial answers

- Curriculum may need to stop when evidence is unresolved

Attribution rule: correlation, high activity, successful decoding, structural density, software adaptation and biological retention are different observations. Keep source attribution and negative controls before upgrading the claim.
