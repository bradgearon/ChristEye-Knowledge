**CE-A23 \| COMPANION • 14 NOV 2026**

Physical Crystal Candidates, Molecular Words and Peptides — Technical Companion

Mechanisms, alternatives, evidence, metrics and engineering handoff

# 01 ChristEye role interpretation

Word: identified signal form; Eyes: receiver; Throne: candidate scaffold; Crystal: hypothesized bounded carrier.

Assignments are scoped engineering roles, not a universal claim that a term is one molecule, cell type or brain region. Physical Soil denotes the living organoid as a whole; external medium and exchange are the Sea.

# 02 Scientific and computational standing

The relevant science supports study of BDNF processing, receptors, PSD condensates, astrocytic states and ECM regulation, but not a portable material engram or behavior-programming peptide.

F = named published finding in its preparation; X = cross-study synthesis; H = untested hypothesis; D = design decision; A = analogy. An untested chain remains H even if its components are established separately.

# 03 Model and quantitative interpretation

## Carrier evidence ladder

> Claim_level = min(level_retention, level_carrier, level_reader, level_causality).

Assumption / applicability: No equation licenses a programmable peptide or a physical engram-transfer claim.

Report independently: Carrier-specific association, content selectivity, delayed benefit, effect vs general activity, matched reader compatibility, uncertainty.

# 04 Data transformation and experiment logic

Input record: ResearchClaim; molecular identity record; carrier hypothesis; selected lab observation

Output record: CrystalCandidateRecord; reader compatibility proposal; hypothesis decision

Implementation decision rule: Track claimed participation as a hypothesis graph; evaluate only lab-supplied measurements; require independent delayed task relationship, carrier association, and selective causal contribution before naming a learned-content carrier.

## Counterfactual checks

- No peptide formula, synthesis, dosing, ingestion or culture addition instructions

- Physical package never has digital installable=true

- An observed structural change is not memory content

# 05 Distinguish competing causes

- Proteolysis changes many processes concurrently

- Material concentration is not semantic storage

- No established biologically encoded book or software-feature transfer

Attribution rule: correlation, high activity, successful decoding, structural density, software adaptation and biological retention are different observations. Keep source attribution and negative controls before upgrading the claim.

Test with a digital simulator first; freeze task and evaluation data separately; publish incomplete or negative findings without renaming them as successes.

# 08 Research and safety boundary

RESEARCH-ONLY. No culture production, growth, stimulation, nutrient dosing, peptide synthesis, ingestion, pathogen modification or medical treatment protocol is authorized.

Qualified laboratories, suppliers and institutional oversight own cell identity, support formulation, living handling and any physical intervention. A physical Crystal or behavior-editing peptide remains hypothetical and has no authorized installation command.
