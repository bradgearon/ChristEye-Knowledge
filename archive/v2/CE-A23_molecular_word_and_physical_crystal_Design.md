**CE-A23 \| DESIGN • 14 NOV 2026**

Physical Crystal Candidates, Molecular Words and Peptides

Subsystem engineering contract / research-only

| **Purpose**        | Organize hypotheses about molecular participation, physical carriers and recipient compatibility without inferring that peptide administration installs knowledge. |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Release stance** | research-only                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Data ownership** | Source-labeled, scope-limited, versioned; no silent biological-state claim.                                                                                        |

# 01 Responsibilities and boundaries

- Distinguish molecular form, receiver, compartment and observed effect

- Keep candidate physical Crystals separate from digital software CrystalPacks

- Record bounded carrier/reader/content/maintenance hypothesis and alternatives

- Require evidence against generic excitability, maturation and external answer-bearing input

Scope boundary: RESEARCH-ONLY. No culture production, growth, stimulation, nutrient dosing, peptide synthesis, ingestion, pathogen modification or medical treatment protocol is authorized.

# 02 Interface contract

| **Inbound**           | ResearchClaim; molecular identity record; carrier hypothesis; selected lab observation       |
|-----------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Outbound**          | CrystalCandidateRecord; reader compatibility proposal; hypothesis decision                   |
| **Connected systems** | A09 living core, A16 roots, A14 consumer, A22 safety                                         |
| **System of record**  | Immutable event ledger and per-module state/claims registry; raw data stay source-addressed. |

# 03 Operational state machine

Required states in declared order (all transitions create a receipt):

1.  Define Carrier

2.  Review Evidence

3.  Describe Reader

4.  Register Controls

5.  Qualified Study

6.  Judge

Transition logic: Track claimed participation as a hypothesis graph; evaluate only lab-supplied measurements; require independent delayed task relationship, carrier association, and selective causal contribution before naming a learned-content carrier.

# 04 Data, observability and model boundary

Measurements: Carrier-specific association, content selectivity, delayed benefit, effect vs general activity, matched reader compatibility, uncertainty.

A module must identify the state owner, event source, observation timestamp, validity flags, schema version, confidence where relevant, and the distinction between proposed and measured behavior.

# 05 Verification and acceptance

- No peptide formula, synthesis, dosing, ingestion or culture addition instructions

- Physical package never has digital installable=true

- An observed structural change is not memory content

Acceptance must be performed on held-out inputs with frozen comparison rules. An unqualified biological preparation cannot inherit a software release result.

# 06 Failure modes and guardrails

- Proteolysis changes many processes concurrently

- Material concentration is not semantic storage

- No established biologically encoded book or software-feature transfer

# 07 Implementation increments

7.  Build claim registry and sample metadata schema

8.  Define hypothetical reader contract

9.  Include source reconciliation and clear research gating

## Exit package

Deliver code/API contracts, deterministic unit tests, a versioned release report, an evidence or risk register, and a machine-readable manifest. Unmeasured biological parameters remain null until externally qualified.
