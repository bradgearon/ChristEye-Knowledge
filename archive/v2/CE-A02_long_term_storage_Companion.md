Long-Term Storage and Consolidation StarChip — Technical Companion

Mechanisms, alternatives, evidence, metrics and engineering handoff

# 01 ChristEye role interpretation

Roots: durable relation; Tree: versioned dependency; Marrow: source lineage; Crystal: digital archive only.

Assignments are scoped engineering roles, not a universal claim that a term is one molecule, cell type or brain region. Physical Soil denotes the living organoid as a whole; external medium and exchange are the Sea.

# 02 Scientific and computational standing

Neural consolidation and digital data durability are different forms of persistence. Delayed biological function is a separate empirical endpoint.

F = named published finding in its preparation; X = cross-study synthesis; H = untested hypothesis; D = design decision; A = analogy. An untested chain remains H even if its components are established separately.

# 03 Model and quantitative interpretation

## Delayed reliability

> Delta_R(t) = P_heldout(t) - P_reference(t).

Assumption / applicability: Do not interpret cryptographic persistence as durable biological memory.

Report independently: Bit-integrity error rate, retrieval accuracy, stale-result rate, snapshot recovery time, topic-shift performance, old-skill noninferiority.

# 04 Data transformation and experiment logic

Input record: EpisodeRecord; document objects; validated findings; Root promotion proposal

Output record: LongTermObject; retrieval index; consolidation receipt; retention report

Implementation decision rule: Stage content under a scoped identity; compute hashes; commit metadata and immutable objects atomically; build indexes; test retrieval after time- and topic-disjoint delays; keep revisions as new nodes rather than overwriting provenance.

## Counterfactual checks

- Reboot and restore preserve source-addressed objects

- Conflicting sources remain distinguishable

- A failed retention probe blocks a biological-memory claim

# 05 Distinguish competing causes

- A storage snapshot is not a neural engram backup

- Index corruption may appear as forgetting

- Repetition may teach the evaluator

Attribution rule: correlation, high activity, successful decoding, structural density, software adaptation and biological retention are different observations. Keep source attribution and negative controls before upgrading the claim.
