# 1 Purpose and boundary

A reusable specialist curriculum generator for any structured discipline: literature, history, economics, physics, medicine-as-education, engineering, philosophy or arts. It produces evaluated competency rather than claiming instant mastery.

# 2 Inputs

- DisciplineStandard with learning outcomes and prerequisites

- Authorized textbooks, papers and laboratory/simulation materials

- Learner/StarChip profile, baseline assessment and intended depth

# 3 Outputs / Winds

- SyllabusGraph, lesson tasks and retrieval prompts

- Verified competency ledger and exam/portfolio artifacts

- Limits of expertise and source coverage report

# 4 Required behavior

- Define foundational and advanced competencies with dependency edges.

- Choose assessment appropriate to the domain: proofs, designs, essays or calibrated predictions.

- Differentiate knowledge of a text from independent professional qualification.

- Keep regulated advice out of scope unless authorized by policy.

# 5 Processing and activation

> **1.** Map discipline outcomes to verifiable demonstrations.
>
> **2.** Import licensed books, exercises and evidence.
>
> **3.** Teach concepts in prerequisite order with reflective comparison.
>
> **4.** Measure unseen transfer and delayed recall.
>
> **5.** Release only the specific demonstrated Wind, not a degree-equivalent claim.

# 6 Test and release gate

- Claims cite tasks and versions rather than a vague intelligence level.

- New syllabus versions retain protected competency tests.

- Theoretical and practical competence are tracked separately.

# 7 Failure and protection

- A model passes exams by memorizing answer keys.

- A college discipline pack is mistaken for licensure or clinical reliability.

# 8 Open research and engineering questions

- Can cross-disciplinary prerequisites be shared without multiplying duplicate lessons?

# 9 Data contract and evidence trace

All calls use versioned request/response schemas. A WindEnvelope records source, recipient, trace_id, data type, delivered time, validity, provenance and uncertainty. Actual hardware commands, if any, require a separate approved permit. Performance claims cite evaluator runs with sealed test sets.
