# 1 Purpose and boundary

Builds research questions, competing hypotheses, causal diagrams, discriminating tests and evidence-update records while keeping speculation separate from measurement.

# 2 Inputs

- ResearchQuestion, background evidence and source provenance

- Known constraints, candidate mechanisms and model assumptions

- Budget for simulation, literature study and experimental review

# 3 Outputs / Winds

- HypothesisSet with predictions and defeaters

- ExperimentPlan at non-procedural conceptual level

- EvidenceUpdate with confidence and rejected alternatives

# 4 Required behavior

- Tag each claim as established finding, synthesis, hypothesis or design.

- Prefer experiments where competing explanations make different predictions.

- Do not convert agreement among generated personas into independent evidence.

- Expose unresolved parameters rather than inventing values.

# 5 Processing and activation

> **1.** Extract observables and a causal graph from the research question.
>
> **2.** Generate at least two rival explanations and relevant confounders.
>
> **3.** Derive discriminating predictions and independent controls.
>
> **4.** Simulate or check existing evidence where tools allow.
>
> **5.** Produce a pre-registration-ready evidence and decision packet.

# 6 Test and release gate

- Every major hypothesis includes a counterexample or failure condition.

- Inferences do not cite a roleplay answer as primary biological data.

- A rejected claim remains traceable in the ledger.

# 7 Failure and protection

- Confirmation bias in literature retrieval.

- Formal notation gives speculative mechanism false authority.

# 8 Open research and engineering questions

- Which ChristEye Word/Move/Judge operations map most cleanly to research actions?

# 9 Data contract and evidence trace

All calls use versioned request/response schemas. A WindEnvelope records source, recipient, trace_id, data type, delivered time, validity, provenance and uncertainty. Actual hardware commands, if any, require a separate approved permit. Performance claims cite evaluator runs with sealed test sets.
