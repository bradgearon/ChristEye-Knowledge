# 1 Purpose and boundary

A small removable digital CrystalPack that makes one authorized book or curated collection available for grounded search, study and demonstration; it is not a peptide-encoded memory copy.

# 2 Inputs

- Authorized title/edition or document collection with rights metadata

- Structure-aware text extraction and citation anchors

- Intended skills, topics and evidence criteria

# 3 Outputs / Winds

- Search index, chapter map, concept graph and reading-plan curriculum

- Grounded answer service with source sections and uncertainty

- Microcrystal version and removal receipts

# 4 Required behavior

- Keep title, edition, translation and provenance exact.

- Separate locating a claim from interpreting it and from learning it.

- Support one-book and many-book composition while keeping conflicts visible.

- Respect quotations and redistribution permissions.

# 5 Processing and activation

> **1.** Import files under rights policy and validate completeness.
>
> **2.** Extract headings, references, figures and concepts.
>
> **3.** Build retrieval index and question coverage matrix.
>
> **4.** Evaluate cited retrieval, comprehension and synthesis on unseen tasks.
>
> **5.** Release an optional study curriculum separate from the index.

# 6 Test and release gate

- A citation identifies the actual edition and passage.

- Conflicting books remain distinguishable rather than silently merged.

- Deleting the pack removes its retrieval capability and caches.

# 7 Failure and protection

- Book context accidentally includes the answer to a sealed test.

- One text corpus treated as an entire mastered discipline.

# 8 Open research and engineering questions

- Can small packs share common prerequisites to speed learning?

# 9 Data contract and evidence trace

All calls use versioned request/response schemas. A WindEnvelope records source, recipient, trace_id, data type, delivered time, validity, provenance and uncertainty. Actual hardware commands, if any, require a separate approved permit. Performance claims cite evaluator runs with sealed test sets.
