# 1 Purpose and boundary

Produces original multitrack compositions with genre-specific language, orchestration constraints, harmonic identity and exportable symbolic tracks; not a player of copyrighted songs.

# 2 Inputs

- MusicBrief: genre, scene, emotion arc, duration range and instrumentation

- Musical grammar packs with rights, feature expectations and evaluation tests

- Optional motif catalog and current adaptive-scoring context

# 3 Outputs / Winds

- CompositionPlan, tempo map, key regions, original motifs

- MIDI-like event streams and stem/arrangement specification

- Assessment of coherence, novelty and harmonic/rhythmic progression

# 4 Required behavior

- Separate compositional representation from audio rendering or voice synthesis.

- Permit deliberate key departure with documented return/resolve rationale.

- Support variation, development and motivic recall across multi-hour works.

- Avoid copying protected melodies or style replicas attributed to one living creator.

# 5 Processing and activation

> **1.** Choose functional genre traits, form and emotional target.
>
> **2.** Generate motive, bass/harmonic scaffold and rhythmic identities.
>
> **3.** Develop orchestration by section and dynamic energy.
>
> **4.** Measure repetition, voice-leading, beat contrast and structural coherence.
>
> **5.** Export editable symbolic tracks for mixing and review.

# 6 Test and release gate

- Output is editable by track and time rather than flattened audio only.

- Melodic hooks remain recognizable after variation.

- Genre adherence and creative distinctiveness are scored separately.

# 7 Failure and protection

- Overfitting to a reference song.

- Novelty metrics reward random notes instead of meaningful divergence.

# 8 Open research and engineering questions

- How should compositional memory coordinate motifs across a game?

# 9 Data contract and evidence trace

All calls use versioned request/response schemas. A WindEnvelope records source, recipient, trace_id, data type, delivered time, validity, provenance and uncertainty. Actual hardware commands, if any, require a separate approved permit. Performance claims cite evaluator runs with sealed test sets.
