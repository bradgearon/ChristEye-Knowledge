Roots Registry, Engram Hypothesis Tracking and Growth Maps — Technical Companion

Mechanisms, alternatives, evidence, metrics and engineering handoff

# 01 ChristEye role interpretation

Roots: validated retained relations; Tree: dependencies and development history; Mesh: typed edges, never automatically promoted.

Assignments are scoped engineering roles, not a universal claim that a term is one molecule, cell type or brain region. Physical Soil denotes the living organoid as a whole; external medium and exchange are the Sea.

# 02 Scientific and computational standing

The registry is the known location of digital Root evidence. Biological engram localization remains hypothetical until selective causal evidence.

F = named published finding in its preparation; X = cross-study synthesis; H = untested hypothesis; D = design decision; A = analogy. An untested chain remains H even if its components are established separately.

# 03 Model and quantitative interpretation

## Protected relation

> Delta R_A = R_A_after_new_learning - R_A_before_new_learning.

Assumption / applicability: A nonsignificant change is not proof of preservation; use noninferiority with a justified margin.

Report independently: Retention horizon, access reliability, edge confidence and type, old-task delta, cross-context support, graph lineage completeness.

# 04 Data transformation and experiment logic

Input record: RootCandidate; delayed task result; Wind route receipts; sensor observations

Output record: RootRecord; typed graph edge; causal-study hypothesis; longitudinal dashboard view

Implementation decision rule: Promote a Root after delayed independent performance; associate episodes and candidate features; measure alternative retrieval and reversible software masking; retain negative results and counterexamples.

## Counterfactual checks

- A candidate region cannot be called memory storage from imaging alone

- Physical contact does not imply meaning or causality

- A missing response leaves storage vs access unresolved

# 05 Distinguish competing causes

- High-dimensional neural drift can scramble naive longitudinal IDs

- Correlated firing reflects common input

- Ablation without power may hide useful routes

Attribution rule: correlation, high activity, successful decoding, structural density, software adaptation and biological retention are different observations. Keep source attribution and negative controls before upgrading the claim.
