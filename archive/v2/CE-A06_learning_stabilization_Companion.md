Learning Stabilization and Consolidation Network — Technical Companion

Mechanisms, alternatives, evidence, metrics and engineering handoff

# 01 ChristEye role interpretation

Roots: delayed task relation; Honey: selective learning feedback; Milk: support; Fruit: retained and transferable performance.

Assignments are scoped engineering roles, not a universal claim that a term is one molecule, cell type or brain region. Physical Soil denotes the living organoid as a whole; external medium and exchange are the Sea.

# 02 Scientific and computational standing

Current organoid learning evidence is task-bounded; a 2026 organoid study reported gains that did not persist after 45 minutes in its tested preparation.

F = named published finding in its preparation; X = cross-study synthesis; H = untested hypothesis; D = design decision; A = analogy. An untested chain remains H even if its components are established separately.

# 03 Model and quantitative interpretation

## Retention with caution

> R_U(d) = (P_delayed(d) - P_chance) / (P_immediate - P_chance).

Assumption / applicability: Use only if the predeclared denominator is sufficiently above zero; R_U may exceed 1 or fall below 0.

Report independently: Absolute delayed macro recall, retention ratio with applicability condition, flexibility, interference change, time-varying calibration.

# 04 Data transformation and experiment logic

Input record: TrainingRun; fixed decoder digest; delayed probes; condition metadata

Output record: RootCandidate; retention vector; interference report; consolidation finding

Implementation decision rule: For each competency freeze the scoring contract; compare acquired performance and delayed retention using appropriate baselines; log every retrieval probe because probes themselves can modify the learner.

## Counterfactual checks

- Delayed score exceeds preregistered baseline across independent units

- Learning and decoder adaptation are explicitly separated

- Old-task protection is assessed as noninferiority, not null-significance

# 05 Distinguish competing causes

- A quiet culture is not a sleeping brain

- Recovery of excitability can mimic retention

- Candidate molecular signal may merely change global responsiveness

Attribution rule: correlation, high activity, successful decoding, structural density, software adaptation and biological retention are different observations. Keep source attribution and negative controls before upgrading the claim.
