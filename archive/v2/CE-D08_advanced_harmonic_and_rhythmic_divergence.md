# 1 Purpose and boundary

An optional expressive-theory StarChip for deliberate departure, tension, release and emotional motion. The user's AHD and ARD formalism can be imported as a separately versioned theory pack when its equations are available.

# 2 Inputs

- Pitch/harmony event stream, beat grid and phrase boundaries

- Target emotional trajectory and tonal/rhythmic home constraints

- Versioned AHD/ARD theory specification with declared variable meanings

# 3 Outputs / Winds

- Tension-contour annotations and candidate harmonic movement

- Rhythmic displacement map and controlled resolution options

- Explanation of deliberate deviation with uncertainty and counterexamples

# 4 Required behavior

- Avoid equating outside-key notes with mere mistakes.

- Keep harmonic interval emotion, temporal buildup and rhythmic divergence distinct.

- Measure structural intent and resolution, not a fake universal emotional score.

- Support side-by-side alternatives that preserve source material rights.

# 5 Processing and activation

> **1.** Parse phrase harmony, rhythm and anticipation points.
>
> **2.** Compute candidate deviations with selected theory version.
>
> **3.** Evaluate constraint satisfaction and perception proxies.
>
> **4.** Offer alternatives emphasizing continuity, surprise or delayed resolution.
>
> **5.** Validate by listening tests rather than algorithmic score alone.

# 6 Test and release gate

- AHD and ARD values are explicitly labeled model-derived.

- Different emotional-curve objectives produce distinguishable arrangements.

- Pack can be disabled without breaking the base composer.

# 7 Failure and protection

- Inventing numerical scores that are not grounded in the supplied AHD/ARD definitions.

- Treating subjective affect as universal biological fact.

# 8 Open research and engineering questions

- Which interval and buildup operators should be imported from final AHD/ARD documentation?

# 9 Data contract and evidence trace

All calls use versioned request/response schemas. A WindEnvelope records source, recipient, trace_id, data type, delivered time, validity, provenance and uncertainty. Actual hardware commands, if any, require a separate approved permit. Performance claims cite evaluator runs with sealed test sets.
