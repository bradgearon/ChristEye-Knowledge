**CE-A14 \| COMPANION • 14 NOV 2026**

Crystal Consumption, Experience Replay and Remanifestation — Technical Companion

Mechanisms, alternatives, evidence, metrics and engineering handoff

# 01 ChristEye role interpretation

Word/Prosody: replay content and order; Water: permitted feed; Roots: independent delayed competence; Crystal: input artifact.

Assignments are scoped engineering roles, not a universal claim that a term is one molecule, cell type or brain region. Physical Soil denotes the living organoid as a whole; external medium and exchange are the Sea.

# 02 Scientific and computational standing

Material carriers added to food/medium do not have a demonstrated method to encode or install arbitrary knowledge. Molecular intake is laboratory research only, not an implementation requirement.

F = named published finding in its preparation; X = cross-study synthesis; H = untested hypothesis; D = design decision; A = analogy. An untested chain remains H even if its components are established separately.

# 03 Model and quantitative interpretation

## Teaching effect

> T = P_B_alone_after - P_B_alone_control on a held-out evaluation after all teaching routes are cleared.

Assumption / applicability: Distinguish active retrieval from changed learner state; software-teacher control is mandatory.

Report independently: Replay completion, retrieval vs learned-skill gap, cold-start gain, delayed score, protected-task loss, rollback success.

# 04 Data transformation and experiment logic

Input record: Signed digital pack; compatible reader; teaching stream; evaluation protocol

Output record: ConsumptionReceipt; ReplayTrace; ActivationState; learned capability result

Implementation decision rule: Install data into a trusted digital staging area (not a culture medium); generate a bounded replay plan; expose only allowed experiences to the target model; clear teacher caches for independent evaluation; retain promotion/rollback history.

## Counterfactual checks

- Unverified packs never train an active module

- Playback cannot bypass test freeze or Heart limits

- Physical consumption through food is not a supported protocol

# 05 Distinguish competing causes

- Replay is not proof of learning

- Model may memorize the answers

- Biological stimulation and feeding must not be conflated

Attribution rule: correlation, high activity, successful decoding, structural density, software adaptation and biological retention are different observations. Keep source attribution and negative controls before upgrading the claim.

# 08 Research and safety boundary

DIGITAL-FIRST; biological claims remain separately qualified. This document provides no physical dosing, feeding, stimulation, cell manufacturing or disease intervention instructions.

Qualified laboratories, suppliers and institutional oversight own cell identity, support formulation, living handling and any physical intervention. A physical Crystal or behavior-editing peptide remains hypothetical and has no authorized installation command.
