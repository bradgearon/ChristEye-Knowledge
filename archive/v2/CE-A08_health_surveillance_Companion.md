**CE-A08 \| COMPANION • 14 NOV 2026**

Health, Anti-Disease Surveillance and Biosafety Network — Technical Companion

Mechanisms, alternatives, evidence, metrics and engineering handoff

# 01 ChristEye role interpretation

Eyes: reliable sensing; Soil: characterized living preparation; Heart: safety review; Milk: mandatory support.

Assignments are scoped engineering roles, not a universal claim that a term is one molecule, cell type or brain region. Physical Soil denotes the living organoid as a whole; external medium and exchange are the Sea.

# 02 Scientific and computational standing

Safeguards are surveillance and escalation only. No engineered pathogen resistance, antiviral/antibacterial modification or biomedical intervention recipe is specified.

F = named published finding in its preparation; X = cross-study synthesis; H = untested hypothesis; D = design decision; A = analogy. An untested chain remains H even if its components are established separately.

# 03 Model and quantitative interpretation

## Alarm quality

> Precision = TP/(TP+FP); Recall = TP/(TP+FN), evaluated at the qualified operating unit.

Assumption / applicability: An alert cannot diagnose an unspecified disease and a missing sensor is not a normal result.

Report independently: Detection latency, false alert burden, sensor validity, traceability completion, review time, within-qualified-band uptime.

# 04 Data transformation and experiment logic

Input record: Environment observations; lab quality release; equipment fault; source and lot record

Output record: HealthAlert; qualification flag; isolation/review request; evidence record

Implementation decision rule: Run software anomaly and rule monitors on qualified observations; classify UNKNOWN when channels are stale or missing; request institutional review for possible biological risk without automatic treatment changes.

## Counterfactual checks

- Stale measurements cannot be treated as normal

- No anti-pathogen engineering or autonomous biological dosing

- Stop/containment actions follow the site-approved safety plan

# 05 Distinguish competing causes

- Anomaly does not diagnose a disease

- Immune-like cells are not automatic biosensors

- Growing more cells can complicate oversight

Attribution rule: correlation, high activity, successful decoding, structural density, software adaptation and biological retention are different observations. Keep source attribution and negative controls before upgrading the claim.

# 07 Build/test checklist

1.  Implement status-only monitoring dashboard

2.  Add stale-data and false-positive tests

3.  Create qualified laboratory escalation interface

Test with a digital simulator first; freeze task and evaluation data separately; publish incomplete or negative findings without renaming them as successes.

# 08 Research and safety boundary

RESEARCH-ONLY. No culture production, growth, stimulation, nutrient dosing, peptide synthesis, ingestion, pathogen modification or medical treatment protocol is authorized.

Qualified laboratories, suppliers and institutional oversight own cell identity, support formulation, living handling and any physical intervention. A physical Crystal or behavior-editing peptide remains hypothetical and has no authorized installation command.
