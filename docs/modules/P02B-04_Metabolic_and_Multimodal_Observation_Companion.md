# P02B-04 · Metabolic and multimodal observation — Scientific and technical companion

**Purpose:** distinguish justified inference, engineering selection and future biological research for [design P02B-04](P02B-04_Metabolic_and_Multimodal_Observation_Design.md).

## Measurable question

Can the declared subsystem perform its specific function and provide independently interpretable data **without** relying on hidden information, unchecked physical changes or overstated biological claims?

**Candidate mechanism or limitation:** Lactate concentration is not a learned fact; astrocytic calcium events are not directly decodable engrams; observing RNA/protein changes does not establish causal retention.

## Alternative explanations and negative cases

lactate confounded by dilution; an optical signal attributed to wrong cell; imaging perturbation; unregistered clocks; nuisance effects mistaken for retention. Model nonacquisition, access impairment, instrumentation failure and physiological support changes as distinct candidate explanations when relevant.

## Evidence program

1. Verify source and unit validity with independent reference data.
2. Freeze relevant analysis/decoder configurations before held-out evaluation.
3. Compare against a simple conventional/digital-only baseline with matched information.
4. Introduce one declared change and measure the entire affected outcome vector, including resource and protected-task cost.
5. Report negative or unresolved outcomes without promoting a hypothesis to finding.

**Concrete acceptance checks:** blank controls; time-skew injection; reporter-lag documentation; different assay lots; held-out prediction; negative-result preservation.

## Biological and hardware qualification

Avoid altering tissue merely to generate labels for a monitoring learner. Nothing in this document substitutes for manufacturer approval, a controlled laboratory preparation, a tissue handling SOP, institutional oversight or independent physical safety review.
