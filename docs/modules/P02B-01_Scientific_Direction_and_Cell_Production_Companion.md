# P02B-01 · Scientific direction and cell production — Scientific and technical companion

**Purpose:** distinguish justified inference, engineering selection and future biological research for [design P02B-01](P02B-01_Scientific_Direction_and_Cell_Production_Design.md).

## Measurable question

Can the declared subsystem perform its specific function and provide independently interpretable data **without** relying on hidden information, unchecked physical changes or overstated biological claims?

**Candidate mechanism or limitation:** A marker profile cannot substitute for electrophysiological or task performance; donor consent, tissue-care SOP and independently approved preparation are prerequisite to living work.

## Alternative explanations and negative cases

source identity incomplete; undetected batch change; evaluator can see sealed labels; software assistant proposes a change as if authorized. Model nonacquisition, access impairment, instrumentation failure and physiological support changes as distinct candidate explanations when relevant.

## Evidence program

1. Verify source and unit validity with independent reference data.
2. Freeze relevant analysis/decoder configurations before held-out evaluation.
3. Compare against a simple conventional/digital-only baseline with matched information.
4. Introduce one declared change and measure the entire affected outcome vector, including resource and protected-task cost.
5. Report negative or unresolved outcomes without promoting a hypothesis to finding.

**Concrete acceptance checks:** missing-signature refusal; frozen-protocol hash validation; source-lot conflict detection; independent batch trace; false finding status blocked.

## Biological and hardware qualification

human subjects consent and genomic privacy; AI-generated protocols cannot authorize production Nothing in this document substitutes for manufacturer approval, a controlled laboratory preparation, a tissue handling SOP, institutional oversight or independent physical safety review.
