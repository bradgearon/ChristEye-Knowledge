# P02B-09 · Neural acquisition and multimodal time base — Scientific and technical companion

**Purpose:** distinguish justified inference, engineering selection and future biological research for [design P02B-09](P02B-09_Neural_Acquisition_and_Multimodal_Time_Base_Design.md).

## Measurable question

Can the declared subsystem perform its specific function and provide independently interpretable data **without** relying on hidden information, unchecked physical changes or overstated biological claims?

**Candidate mechanism or limitation:** Synchronicity and correlation do not prove a synapse or learned semantic relation. Extra state and feedback invalidate simplistic Markov information arguments.

## Alternative explanations and negative cases

clock domain mismatch; dropped samples; timestamp derived from browser display; optical reporter lag treated as spike timing; unsorted electrodes called unique cells. Model nonacquisition, access impairment, instrumentation failure and physiological support changes as distinct candidate explanations when relevant.

## Evidence program

1. Verify source and unit validity with independent reference data.
2. Freeze relevant analysis/decoder configurations before held-out evaluation.
3. Compare against a simple conventional/digital-only baseline with matched information.
4. Introduce one declared change and measure the entire affected outcome vector, including resource and protected-task cost.
5. Report negative or unresolved outcomes without promoting a hypothesis to finding.

**Concrete acceptance checks:** clock skew and jitter; synthetic dropout; bridge round-trip; record retention; channel-map revision; frozen decoder digest.

## Biological and hardware qualification

Vendor supports physical acquisition/stimulation; no custom high-voltage or electrode-driver board is released. Nothing in this document substitutes for manufacturer approval, a controlled laboratory preparation, a tissue handling SOP, institutional oversight or independent physical safety review.
