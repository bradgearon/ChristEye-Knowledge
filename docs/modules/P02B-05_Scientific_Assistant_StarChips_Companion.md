# P02B-05 · Scientific assistant StarChips — Scientific and technical companion

**Purpose:** distinguish justified inference, engineering selection and future biological research for [design P02B-05](P02B-05_Scientific_Assistant_StarChips_Design.md).

## Measurable question

Can the declared subsystem perform its specific function and provide independently interpretable data **without** relying on hidden information, unchecked physical changes or overstated biological claims?

**Candidate mechanism or limitation:** Generated biological mechanisms are hypotheses. Independent laboratory validation and prospectively locked evidence are separate stages.

## Alternative explanations and negative cases

AI conflates Star dialogue with real data; invents a cell recipe; claims a theoretical model is clinically validated; hallucinates source or timing. Model nonacquisition, access impairment, instrumentation failure and physiological support changes as distinct candidate explanations when relevant.

## Evidence program

1. Verify source and unit validity with independent reference data.
2. Freeze relevant analysis/decoder configurations before held-out evaluation.
3. Compare against a simple conventional/digital-only baseline with matched information.
4. Introduce one declared change and measure the entire affected outcome vector, including resource and protected-task cost.
5. Report negative or unresolved outcomes without promoting a hypothesis to finding.

**Concrete acceptance checks:** blinded source attribution; unanswerable prompts; conflicting evidence; refusal of unapproved actuation; source-paragraph audit.

## Biological and hardware qualification

No direct manufacture, dosing, surgical or tissue-control authority for scientist agents. Nothing in this document substitutes for manufacturer approval, a controlled laboratory preparation, a tissue handling SOP, institutional oversight or independent physical safety review.
