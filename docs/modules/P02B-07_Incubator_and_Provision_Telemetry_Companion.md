**Purpose:** distinguish justified inference, engineering selection and future biological research for [design P02B-07](P02B-07_Incubator_and_Provision_Telemetry_Design.md).

## Measurable question

Can the declared subsystem perform its specific function and provide independently interpretable data **without** relying on hidden information, unchecked physical changes or overstated biological claims?

**Candidate mechanism or limitation:** Essential support is independent of task success. Milk is not an reward channel or a permission to experiment with deprivation.

## Alternative explanations and negative cases

remote reservoir mistaken for at-cell pH; absent service assumed normal; learning score alters feeding; unauthorized automated pump command. Model nonacquisition, access impairment, instrumentation failure and physiological support changes as distinct candidate explanations when relevant.

## Evidence program

1. Verify source and unit validity with independent reference data.
2. Freeze relevant analysis/decoder configurations before held-out evaluation.
3. Compare against a simple conventional/digital-only baseline with matched information.
4. Introduce one declared change and measure the entire affected outcome vector, including resource and protected-task cost.
5. Report negative or unresolved outcomes without promoting a hypothesis to finding.

**Concrete acceptance checks:** unplugged OEM port; stale sample; conflicting redundant sensors; service record without physical receipt; host shutdown.
