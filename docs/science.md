# Scientific director, cell foundry and independent review

## Scientific director (human PI)

Owns study questions, designs, inclusion/exclusion rules, comparison units, analysis plans, preregistration, amendment decisions and claim language. Approves transitions between evidence gates but cannot waive independent care/safety constraints.

## Cell production and development scientist

Owns qualified source-bank, donor/lineage permissions where applicable, incoming lot characterization, source/development history, cell identities, maturation release criteria, batch deviations, care SOPs and operator handoff to the instrument team.

Production is a **qualified laboratory workflow**, not an automated action triggered by a StarChip. A mature learner is not an uncontrolled continuously expanding progenitor bank.

## Additional review

| Discipline | Distinct contribution |
|---|---|
| Electrophysiology | electrode maps, artifacts, valid readouts and stimulation qualification |
| Astrocyte/neural biology | cellular identity, development and cross-cell claims |
| Microscopy | registration, spatial resolution, reporter kinetics and phototoxicity |
| Metabolomics/proteomics | assay identity, recovery, flux and interpretation |
| Biostatistics | preparation-level inference, controls, uncertainty and power |
| Embedded/EE | timing, power, EMI, isolation and board fault assessment |
| Reliability & biosafety | containment, independent permits, care continuity and retirement |

## Scientist StarChips

**Research Director StarChip:** a digital research *assistant* producing falsifiable alternatives, test proposals, citations and limitation records.

**Cell Foundry StarChip:** a digital assistant maintaining qualified source/lot/measurement records and flagging missing release evidence.

Neither is granted autonomous biological manufacturing, chemical dosing or tissue-care permission.

## Evidence register

Record claim ID, preparation, source publication or dataset, interpretation, evidence label (F/X/H/D/M), independent tests, conflicting explanations, review signature and dated revision.
