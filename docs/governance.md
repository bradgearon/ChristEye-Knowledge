## Roles and approval

The scientific director approves claims and experiments; the qualified culture lead owns sourcing and care. An independent hardware limit controller applies physical vetoes that no StarChip can override. Experimental observations remain distinct from validated product claims.

## Biological boundaries

No new cell types, experimental chemical mixtures, feed schedules or electrode stimulation envelopes may be inferred from software scores or metaphors. Living work requires relevant source consent, scientific/biosafety oversight, vendor-specific qualification, controlled SOPs and independent documentation. All material support remains independent of task success.

## Data governance

Permissioned usage capture; explicit provenance for books and external content; no automatic ingestion of private files. Retain versions and negative results. Exclude donor genomes and personally identifying logs from this public site. Remove patent drafts and claims from public release artifacts.

## Rights and limitations

Publishing research does not establish patent protection or grant unrestricted use. Third-party content continues under its source license. Financial, biological, medical and consciousness claims require separate evidence.
