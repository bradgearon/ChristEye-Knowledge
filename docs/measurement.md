# Measurement and Root-evidence architecture

Instrumentation records **what was measured**, distinct from claims about learned content or consciousness.

| Channel | Possible observation | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| HD-MEA | spikes, bursts, latency, evoked-response features | electrodes are not neuron identities; artifact and map drift |
| Environmental | temperature, pH, osmolality, fluid status | location and freshness matter |
| Metabolic | glucose/lactate samples or qualified biosensors | concentration does not uniquely reveal flux or a memory |
| Astrocytic optical | cell-specific calcium/reporters | not interchangeable with spike trains or content |
| RNA/proteins | selected reporters, transcript/protein assays | incomplete spatial coverage, perturbing assays, turnover |
| Morphology | cell distributions, neurites, selected dendritic spines | shape/contact cannot establish learned function |
| Matrix | selected extracellular structures or local processing | not a universal plasticity “switch” |
| Behavioral | timed accuracy, macro recall, transfer, calibration | digital decoder and biological contribution must be distinguished |

A **RootRecord** ties: preparation ID, cue/context, task/reader digest, baseline, training history, delayed assessment, uncertainty, relevant structural and molecular observations, competing causes and status. Failed performance can mean access loss, measurement faults, general support effects or loss of retention; record **UNRESOLVED** when these cannot be distinguished.

### Data practices

Use self-describing time series and immutable event receipts. Consider [Neurodata Without Borders](https://www.nwb.org/) for neural/behavioral recordings and documented microscopy archive conventions. A visual or biochemical signal is evidence of an observation, not automatic evidence for a stored sentence or physical Crystal.
