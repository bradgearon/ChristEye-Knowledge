# The next Anaiero website — site development plan

**November 15, 2026 · WDS research property**

The current public website is being expanded into a professional cross-disciplinary atlas of the Anaiero architecture, its developer interfaces, neuroscience, growth and care processes, scientific evidence, theoretical scholarship and grounded StarChip demonstrations.

[Read the complete Step 1 source audit and blueprint](../website/README.md).

This work begins with a detailed site information architecture and content map. It maps **352 existing source files** to proposed native website routes, including the 24 design/companion pairs, biological process literature, schemas and specialist chip manifests.

The existing site continues to publish while the new source-rendering system is developed and validated. The next technical vertical slice is CE-A02 Long-Term Storage with a full design, scientific companion, original schemas, spinal Root/axon process links and a reproducible digital Crystal replay.

This is a **website development roadmap**, not a claim of a complete living computer. The WDS Public Research Reference License applies and the separate patent document is not included.
