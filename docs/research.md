## Interpretation limits

The source texts include untested models, some later superseded or mathematically corrected. The [Formal Models and Reconciliation](../research/full-markdown/R03_03_Formal_Models_and_Reconciliation_1_.md) is the preferred audit for correcting incompatible claims. An organoid responding to a bounded stimulus task does not establish a complete mind. Evidence of specific molecular processes is not evidence that a peptide carries a learned semantic fact.

The project uses a versioned scientific evidence ladder: finding (F), synthesis (X), hypothesis (H), design (D), and analogy (A). Claimed living results require preparation identity and independent data.
