## Interpretation rules

- A cited paper supports only its measured system, task and preparation.
- A formula may be valid mathematics without being a validated biological law.
- The original training dialogue and generated personas supply *hypothesis prompts*, not independent experimental observations.
- Findings on mouse memory, spinal circuits or planar cultures cannot silently certify a human 3D organoid.
- A physical Crystal requires a bounded carrier, selective content evidence, compatible reader and independent controls; digital replay does not establish biochemical storage.
