# Digital Crystals, Roots and replay

**Digital CrystalPack (software):** a versioned package of curricula, examples, retrieval references, source hashes, replay instructions, dependency contracts, behavioral assessments and declared rights.

**Physical Crystal (research):** a hypothetical bounded material carrier that preserves a particular acquired relationship for a compatible reader. No such general transferable learned-knowledge mechanism is established by this project.

### Package manifest contract

An installation declares identity, version, provenance, content classification, rights, dependency graph, prerequisites, target interfaces, expected outcomes, recorded training episodes, frozen assessments and rollback scope. “Clone” means a byte-verified digital copy, not duplication of a learned biological state.

### Replay meanings

A replay may *show* episodes, provide supervision, reconstruct a digital index or stimulate a simulated learner. Replaying neuronal spikes into another biological preparation does not establish transferred Understanding. A **recipient-only** delayed test, with live teacher routes and shared adaptive state cleared, is required for independent acquisition claims.

### Molecular provisions

Peptides, BDNF-related processing and molecular Words may influence signaling or receiving state in particular preparations. They are **not** validated storage for arbitrary semantic memories or executable feature upgrades; biological interventions require separately qualified research review.

### Memory preservation

Maintain a separation between archived software data, measured delayed Roots, inferred material correlates and physical carriers. Any system output that calls itself a Crystal must name which of these it represents.
