### R06: Robbins et al., Goal-directed learning in cortical organoids, Cell Reports 2026

[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41720084/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41720084/)

**Applicable support:** Adaptive feedback learning on task, but no retained improvement after tested 45-minute rest.

### R07: Cai et al., Brain organoid reservoir computing for artificial intelligence, Nature Electronics 2023

[https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-023-01069-w](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-023-01069-w)

**Applicable support:** A bounded hybrid neural-computing precedent, not a full semantic mind.

### R08: Williamson et al., Learning-associated astrocyte ensembles regulate memory recall, Nature 2025

[https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08170-w](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08170-w)

**Applicable support:** Mouse in-vivo astrocyte recall evidence; does not establish organoid memory storage.

### R09: Dewa et al., The astrocytic ensemble acts as a multiday trace to stabilize memory, Nature 2025

[https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09619-2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09619-2)

**Applicable support:** Mouse slow-state stabilization candidate; nonportable to organoid without tests.

### R10: Taga et al., Human spinal astrocyte influence on motor neuron maturation, Stem Cells Translational Medicine 2019

[https://doi.org/10.1002/sctm.19-0147](https://doi.org/10.1002/sctm.19-0147)
