# CE-A02 · Animon Long-Term Retention — Scientific Companion v3.2

**Primary scientific question Q02:** Could a future qualified living Animon candidate acquire a bounded history-dependent relation and **demonstrate its delayed use** with the teaching and answer-bearing channels removed, a frozen independent reader, adequate physiological support and valid controls?

A **functional Root** is the observed ability to use a particular relation at a specified horizon; a **spinal Root tissue** is a proposed source/structure. A spinal contribution or unique location of the learned relation is a separate hypothesis requiring CE-A16's anatomical and functional evidence, not simply a performance score. See [ANIMON/1](../../../ontology/ANIMON_V1.md), [A02 engineering design](../design/CE-A02_Animon_Long_Term_Retention_Design.md) and [A16 scientific companion](CE-A16_Animon_Spinal_Root_Scientific_Companion.md).

**Present outcome:** NOT TESTED IN LIVING ANIMON. The repository contains software retrieval, digital CrystalPack replay and retention-metric functions; it does not contain an evaluated biological Root dataset.

## 1. Three levels of retention claim

| Claim class | What would be demonstrated | Evidence that is insufficient |
|---|---|---|
| Digital persistence and retrieval | Verified source bytes, rights and a stable retrieval under a new reader/context | Arbitrary competence or biological memory |
| Functional retained relation | A declared task's selective delayed performance with actual exposure, frozen reader and matched controls | Immediate improvement, bulk firing, source index recovery |
| Physical biological storage mechanism | Candidate molecular/circuit source contributes to the task beyond health, access and digital reader, with independently justified causal evidence | Protein expression, higher spike rate, axon density or correlated traces |
| Portable physical carrier (still stronger) | A bounded physical carrier and compatible recipient demonstrate content-specific, source-independent acquisition | Physical structural association or a digital Crystal copied by SHA-256 |

Every claim must be attributable to an **actual preparation**, experimental unit, test context and source/assay. A digital Animon reference completing a task does not establish a living individual.

## 4. Acquisition, retention, access and task generalization are different

A meaningful task relation first needs validated acquisition: a learner's response to a declared input/context has changed relative to an appropriate baseline and relevant matched controls. A **delayed** assessment then asks whether selective performance persists *without answer-bearing teaching* at a later horizon.

Separate these questions:
- **Learning:** did historical exposure change the response relation? Time-matched maturation and random-label/permutation baselines matter.
- **Access:** did the available cue, readout, routing or decoding change after the learning period?
- **Retention:** did the relevant relation remain usable after a real interval with the reader held constant?
- **Transfer:** does the relation extend to defined new examples without memorizing them from previous exposure?
- **Protection:** were previously demonstrated skills maintained within a scientifically justified comparison margin?
- **Biological contribution:** can a particular source/axon/molecular factor actually be implicated beyond general health, alternative input and host computation?

A high immediate score does not answer the other five. A deleted digital index does not demonstrate loss of living memory. A new physically observed axon does not prove new Understanding.

## 5. Primary endpoint and honest uncertainty

For the three-class X, Y, HOLD reference task, the existing [anaiero/roots.py](../../../anaiero/roots.py) computes **macro recall** from the three per-class recall values:

\[
M=\frac{1}{3}\sum_{c\in\{X,Y,\mathrm{HOLD}\}}
 \frac{TP_c}{N_c},
\qquad N_c >0.
\]

A constant HOLD strategy has a per-class pattern that can be distinguished from real selectivity. Report the confusion matrix, source/decoder validity, class counts, missing/invalid trials and nonlearning comparators.

**Potential study-level primary effect** (subject to qualified statistical review):

\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{delay}} =
 \overline{M_{\mathrm{contingent,delayed}}} -
 \overline{M_{\mathrm{matched\ control,delayed}}},
\]

where averages must be defined over the actual **independent biological units**, not falsely treating thousands of trials from one preparation as independent cultures.

The project has *not* selected a real preparation or sample-size rationale, so no numeric effect threshold, power calculation or statistical significance target is asserted. A data engineer may implement the formula; a lab statistician must qualify endpoints, exclusions, uncertainty and the biological hierarchy before a real study.

## 6. Retention ratio and interpretation limits

The current digital metric can produce:

\[
R=\frac{M_{\mathrm{delayed}}-M_{\mathrm{chance}}}
{M_{\mathrm{immediate}}-M_{\mathrm{chance}}},
\]

provided the immediate result exceeds its declared chance margin. If the denominator is weak or zero, retention is **unqualified**. A negative R or R greater than one can occur mathematically; R must not be presented as a probability or a percent of remaining neurons. The **absolute delayed performance and uncertainty** remain primary.

The same measured performance can be modeled by an unobserved retained capacity K and momentary access A:

\[
P_{\mathrm{observed}} =
 P_{\mathrm{chance}} + (K-P_{\mathrm{chance}})A.
\]

This conceptual factorization has **multiple possible K/A explanations for one score**. Without independently varied access/reader conditions, one cannot infer that poor output means erased memory or that better access is new synaptic learning.

## 7. Controls, alternative explanations and non-identifiability

| Observed result | At least one alternative | Proposed scientific discrimination |
|---|---|---|
| Training period performance rises | change in the adaptive host reader or task difficulty | fixed codebook/decoder, source-verified inputs, matched comparator |
| Delayed score falls | access/reader drift, physiological state change, source loss | independent source integrity, care validity and alternate cue with *no new training* |
| Digital Crystal replay succeeds | correct lexical retrieval rather than independently learned generalization | independently authored held-out task and no answer-bearing source at evaluation |
| New axonal track appears | maturation or imaging/segmentation artifact | source and age controls, registered structural validity and independently supported functional route |
| Structural scaffold assay increases | generic excitability, cell health or molecular turnover | specific molecular and functional control, not only abundance |
| Candidate Root performs only while a teacher is joined | assisted use, hidden cached answer, shared adaptive state | recipient-only test after actual teaching route/caches removed |
| Multiple electrodes show synchronized activity | shared field, common stimulation or processing pipeline | qualified channel-level physical and statistical controls |
| Old task score decreases | interference, medium/viability change, new decoder | protected old-task assessment, independent support and source tracking |

No software system may approve physical growth, nutrient or peptide changes based solely on a metric. The role of the companion is to define what *must be distinguished*, not how to implement live biological interventions.

## 8. Measurement ontology and data trace

An actual living retention claim would require the following chain:

**Source preparation and consent → care and viability qualification → actual delivered input/cue receipts → locked codebook and evaluator → immediate labeled trial results → defined later interval (with feedback off) → delayed independent trial results → source/batch-level analysis → separate axon/molecular mechanism evidence → scientific review.**

Each arrow requires real evidence or explicitly UNRESOLVED. Sampling position, timestamp/clock-domain alignment, chemistry calibration, image coverage and raw asset digest are part of interpretation. A missing sample is not proof of unchanged physiology.

The [FunctionalRootAssessment schema](../../../schemas/biology/functional_root_assessment.schema.json) is used for the proposed outcome record. Its [synthetic unobserved example](../../../fixtures/v3.2/functional_root_unobserved.example.json) deliberately contains **no scores**, no teaching receipt, and a dummy all-zero reader digest; never represent that digest as an actual frozen model artifact.

## 9. Physiological care and limitations

SustiBus is an independent ongoing life-support status and service record, not task reward. An Animon proposal cannot have a training system that withholds essential support after failure; the scientific reader must distinguish support/culture change and the intended learning mechanism. See [CE-A17 care companion](CE-A17_Animon_Care_Scientific_Companion.md).

The 2011 rat hippocampal lactate finding concerns real metabolic support in its biological model. It is **not** a source-qualified Animon feeding protocol and does not identify a molecular packet containing a book. Likewise, protein-turnover/memory observations do not establish an installable physical Crystal.

## 10. What would falsify each increasingly strong claim?

1. **Acquisition** fails if a matched control explains the improvement under a fixed evaluator.
2. **Retention** is unsupported if later valid performance is indistinguishable from its comparator within the specified condition and uncertainty.
3. **Unique spinal contribution** is unsupported if structural/functional evidence does not distinguish the nominated spinal region from unrelated or shared-input routes.
4. **Molecular mechanism** is unsupported if molecular changes track generalized viability/excitability rather than a specific functional contribution.
5. **Portable material carrier** remains unsupported until a bounded, compatible reader and *recipient-only* content-specific acquisition have independent evidence.
6. **General Animon cognition or sentience** is beyond the supported outcomes, regardless of the outcome of a narrow task.

Negative findings are valid science and belong in the evidence record. A project may continue theorizing only by updating its hypothesis and limitations, not by describing the same data as a stronger result.
