# CE-A09 · Animon Core — Scientific Companion v3.2 pilot

**This companion is the biological interpretation of the [CE-A09 design](../design/CE-A09_Animon_Core_Design.md), not an executable protocol.** The proposed individual **Animon** is the larger developmental system; the candidate **Soil** organoid is a physical preparation within that proposal. A digital Animon reference is neither alive nor a biological experimental unit. [ANIMON/1](../../../ontology/ANIMON_V1.md).

## 1. Primary scientific question and competing hypotheses

**Question Q09:** In a future qualified neural preparation used as candidate Soil, is a reported task-selective response attributable to history-dependent changes within the biological network rather than external decoding, source maturation, artifact or generalized physiological stress?

| ID | Classification | Testable explanation | What would weaken it |
|---|---|---|---|
| H09-A | H — Animon proposal | A preparation-specific neural response changes systematically with declared learning history | Response is indistinguishable from time-matched noncontingent exposure |
| H09-B | H — support alternative | A changed readout reflects cell health/exchange or astrocyte support rather than task content | Content selectivity remains after accounting for qualified support variation |
| H09-C | H — digital alternative | Host encoder/decoder adaptation produces the apparent improvement | Fixed-reader performance remains different in recipient-only evaluation |
| H09-D | H — geometry alternative | Apparent network routes are shared fields/correlation, not specific axonal influence | Registered source-target routes lack independent functional validation |
| D09-E | D — engineering necessity | True learning evidence requires valid source, clock, instrumentation and sealed comparator | Missingness, label leakage or unqualified source makes interpretation unresolved |

**Current outcome:** Q09 is **NOT TESTED IN ANIMON**. The project registry is a plan, not a local dataset. A beautiful Soil illustration or spontaneous calcium/spike network is not a task result.

## 3. Which biological participants could matter?

**Neuron and regional identity:** an approved preparation-specific panel may include MAP2, TUBB3 and selected spinal-derived subtype markers. These tell us about differentiated phenotype or structure only within a named source and assay. An isolated marker or bulk transcriptomics measurement is insufficient to infer stable long-range circuit function.

**Astrocyte and glial support:** SOX9, ALDH1L1 and GFAP may contribute to glial identity assessment. Astrocytic metabolite transport and ionic/glutamate regulation are candidate *support mechanisms*, not a physical semantic storage codec. The actual population source and spatial relationship require laboratory verification.

**Axonal projections:** a neurite’s anatomical path is a component of a neuron, not another cell. A proposed spinal Root association must be separated into structural, statistical, intervention-supported and task-specific relationships. [BIO-02](../../../processes/BIO-02-axon-spinal-root.md).

**Crest origin:** source lot and observed lineage are separate. Assigned cell_instance_id values denote image/assay observations, not necessarily genetically encoded IDs. A proposed individual Animon identity is distinct from every cell’s lineage.

## 4. Measurement and validity matrix

| Proposed readout | Independent quantity / unit | Hidden confound | Evidence required before interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Region and tissue structure | qualified spatial transform, volume and fraction covered, uncertainty | central region not actually imaged | explicit unobserved volume and image/assay reference |
| Cells and lineage | sampled phenotypes, observed tracks and source lot | segmentation and barcode collision/dropout | multi-marker and lineage confidence, validation reference |
| Axon/synapse | registered candidate structure, terminal context and possibly latency | electrode co-activity/common electrical field | morphology **plus separate functional control** |
| Electrical response | channel/ROI, timing, artifacts and input receipt | stimulus artifact, decoder drift, common drive | frozen reader and calibrated source/recipient route |
| Chemical state | analyte/form, location, units, timing, recovery | production versus uptake versus dilution | calibrated sampled chemistry and service history |
| Learning task | per-class response, task source, immediate/delayed score | answer leakage, habituation, disease/stress | sealed evaluator, matched training and actual delayed probe |
| Care continuity | real qualified service receipt and environment status | missing sensor interpreted as healthy | independent care plan, source SOP and freshness status |

**Observation scope must be clear.** Transcript/protein measurements may be region-, cell- or sample-level and frequently destructive; they are not continuous readouts of all RNA in every living cell. Optics cannot infer an organoid's unobserved interior merely because surface cells are visible.

## 5. Quantification without invented results

For *a predeclared task metric* M, a simple experimental contrast might be written as:

\[
\Delta M = M_{\text{contingent condition}} - M_{\text{matched noncontingent control}}.
\]

The metric units, independent number of preparations, confidence interval, timing and multiple-comparison handling must come from the *actual study design*. Treating thousands of electrodes or trials from one preparation as thousands of independent biological samples is invalid pseudoreplication.

For optical coverage, if a volume-of-interest V has a defensibly characterized observable subset V_obs, one may report:

\[
C_{\text{coverage}} = \frac{V_{\text{observed and validated}}}{V_{\text{region of interest}}}
\]

with **the measurement method, spatial transform, missing interior and uncertainty**, never as a fabricated 100%. Coverage does not measure sentience, knowledge or maturity.

For a delayed response, separately report score relative to chance and known task-class prevalence; a constant HOLD choice must not be promoted as context understanding. The [v3.1 A02 formal discussion](../../v3/companion/CE-A02_long_term_storage_Companion.md) provides the complementary distinction between apparent retention and access.

## 6. Falsification and alternatives

The Animon Core mechanism is not supported if:

- training and no-training matched controls yield indistinguishable valid task differences;
- the benefit disappears with a frozen evaluator while a mutable host decoder alone improves;
- apparently task-specific network activity is explained by time, tissue health or source/care change;
- an asserted spinal pathway cannot be distinguished from common-input/cross-channel artifacts;
- a model cannot supply actual preparation/assay calibration and ethical source provenance.

A negative result in one preparation is **not** evidence that all living computation is impossible; it narrows this claim, preparation and endpoint. Conversely, a positive result in an external organoid does not automatically instantiate an Animon.

## 7. Independent care, human ethics and the meaning of development

A proposed living substrate requires qualified environmental and nutritional support regardless of its score. The *Animon* name emphasizes stewardship and developmental history, but the term does not show the system has emotions or experiences "love." Love is a WDS design value; the observable care requirement is actual maintenance continuity with a responsible cell lead. See [CE-A17 companion v3.2](CE-A17_Animon_Care_Scientific_Companion.md).

A proposed chemical/growth change is an **independently reviewed research question**. No paper in this companion supplies a universal food recipe or method to engineer an Animon-specific new brain.

## 8. Scientific handoff decision

Before an actual experiment, the designated institution must select a source/model, approve relevant SOPs, establish sample and instrument validity, declare the experimental unit and statistical comparison, and record independent care/safety responsibility. A **StudyReadinessPacket** with unresolved values stays DRAFT/LAB_REVIEW_PENDING.

**Current finding:** no Anaiero living Animon data were provided in this repository review. The next scientifically honest deliverable is a qualified *proposal ready for human review*, not a claim that the Animon has learned or been created.

## V3.3 scientist procedure — how a Core claim would be tested and rejected

**Primary scientific question:** does the actual chosen source preparation contain the asserted regional neuron/glia identity and a qualified response beyond ordinary source development, sample handling or the electronic reader?

1. **Select source** using [WO-00](../../../laboratory/WO-00-source-and-genomic-qc.md). Request relevant source-line genetic identity report, genome stability and contamination/phenotype certificates, with donor privacy restrictions. Document source-line differences and actual independent biological units.
2. **Record first authorized observation** using [WO-01](../../../laboratory/WO-01-soil-development-and-maturation.md). Capture actual preparation and spatial region, stage, raw images, viability method, assay control and surface/deep coverage.
3. **At lab-approved stage milestones**, obtain the source-appropriate RNA/protein/structural cell-state evidence under the institution SOP. Candidate panels include source/context-dependent neuronal, spinal and glial markers; no single marker determines a fully functioning circuit.
4. **Compare to the right reference:** previous valid same-preparation observation *and* stage/source-matched independent preparations. Check whether a medium, source, imaging or readout method changed and whether an interior region is unobserved.
5. **Before any task claim**, request independently qualified I/O instrumentation, care and cue/decoder reference; report whether the task effect could be source maturation or host decoder adaptation.
6. **Issue a source-scoped result:** SOURCE_QUALIFIED, REGION_OBSERVED, CELL_IDENTITY_SUPPORTED, FUNCTIONAL_BASELINE_SUPPORTED, NEGATIVE or UNRESOLVED. No living-Animon maturity conclusion follows from a beautiful organoid shape alone.

**Required scientist deliverable:** [source-release form](../../../laboratory/forms/SOURCE_RELEASE_FORM.md), raw sample/assay provenance, stage comparison chart, actual care/incident status, and signed source-specific interpretation. The separate P06 3D Soil model must never inherit the R1 planar cell counts or claimed chemistry without a new laboratory qualification.
