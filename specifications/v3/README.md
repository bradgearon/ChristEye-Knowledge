## Implementation maturity

- **Buildable now (digital):** versioned lessons, consented memory/CrystalPacks, retrieval and filesystem, WindBus messages, simulated supervisors, audit and frozen evaluation pipelines.
- **Bench qualification required:** actual MCU boards, vendor neural acquisition/optic/chemistry interfaces and independent safety.
- **Separately approved laboratory research:** source and Crest lineage, 3D Soil maturity, spinal Root tissue, axonal connection, candidate tissue-linked learning and chemical modulation.
- **No demonstrated method:** an autonomous complete biological mind, semantics encoded in food/peptides, physical Crystal with reproducible portable learned knowledge, and per-daughter globally unique genetic IDs.

The existing supplier-specific planar cell counts are in [P01 original record](../../phases/P01/docs/01_Physical_Module_Composition_and_Counts.md) and are not transferred to 3D organoids or individual digital StarChips.

## Scientific and ethical boundary

No file here instructs an AI to fabricate, feed or treat living tissue. Any operator-facing lab method and physical parameter must be chosen/qualified by the specific laboratory and signed owners; unknowns are explicit gates. The **confidential physical patent document is not included**, but WDS-owned experiments and non-patent designs are part of the research corpus.
