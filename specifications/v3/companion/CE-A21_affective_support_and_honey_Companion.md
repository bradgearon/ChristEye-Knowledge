# CE-A21 — Affective Support And Honey · Scientific companion

**Revision 3.1** · [Engineering design](../design/CE-A21_affective_support_and_honey_Design.md) · [Evidence ledger](../../../research/evidence/anaiero-biology-2026-11.md)

## 1. Testable claim at the correct physical scale

**Proposed ordinary substrate:** digital compassionate support and separate task learning feedback.

**Module capability to explain:** consent → user support policy → scoped feedback → independent task outcome/experience evaluation.

For a living claim, define a particular preparation, task relation, independent receiving/teacher channels, readout, delayed evaluation horizon and result. The original engineering roles **do not** declare that a named molecule *is* the Word, Heart, Honey, Soul, Root or Lotus; these are testable role assignments with measured physical participants.

## 2. Source-backed candidate participants

**Unique candidates for CE-A21:** neuromodulatory and astrocytic signaling as observation candidates only.

| Mechanistic class | Scientific interpretation to test | Minimum discriminator |
|---|---|---|
| Cellular identity | actual sourced neuron/glia/progenitor subtypes in this preparation, not generic "brain cells" | multi-marker identity, source batch and region |
| Axonal architecture | do not infer human emotions from neural spikes or peripheral axon activity | registered morphology AND a separate functional influence test |
| Receptor/signaling | ordinary candidate forms named above, within the receptor/compartment where reported | distinguish effect from generalized excitability |
| Structural maintenance | scaffold, astrocyte and ECM state if implicated by the candidate hypothesis | delayed relation-specific effect with controlled viability |
| Nutrient/exchange | source-qualified substrate and selected transport metabolites; care remains task independent | calibration and supply/uptake/dilution alternatives |
| Digital storage/replay | source-hashed episodes and immutable model/filesystem state | independent software restoration, not biological engram claim |

Relevant literature: [2026 organoid readouts B01](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44222-026-00507-6), [2026 corticospinal connectoid B02](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42201810/), [2025 sensory assembloid B03](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08808-3), [2011 lactate-memory study B07](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3073831/). None asserts the project has reproduced those results.

## 3. Axon and spinal Root hypotheses

Treat **Root tissue** as a candidate spinal-derived system and **learned Root** as a delayed retained relation. They are linked by an explicit evidence edge only after morphological, functional and task-specific comparisons; never by the metaphor "spine" alone. Axonal projections are neuronal processes; morphological continuity is not equivalent to directed transmission or retained knowledge. [BIO-02](../../../processes/BIO-02-axon-spinal-root.md).

**Study-specific axon question:** do not infer human emotions from neural spikes or peripheral axon activity. Record source identity, target identity, imaging coverage, timing and field/electrode artifacts, preparation uncertainty, competing common-drive routes and reproducibility across truly independent biological units.

## 4. Cells, Crest and identity

The Crest is an audited cell-source and maturation service; production requires qualified human SOP and source consent. Proposed cell tracking uses source_lot_id, clone_lineage_id, per-observation cell_instance_id and track_id/confidence. **Clone identity does not preserve learned state.** A heritable barcode can identify a shared ancestry; collision/dropout and perturbation make it unsuitable as a presumed unique baby-cell ID. [CellTag-multi (2024)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-023-01931-4), [lineage tracing limits (2025)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11996212/).

## 5. Acquisition, Root retention and competing causes

**Candidate staged test:** consent → user support policy → scoped feedback → independent task outcome/experience evaluation.

Predeclare:
- T1: prospective improvement under declared contingent teaching compared with matched noncontingent input and source maturation.
- T2: delayed selective performance with all answer-bearing teaching routes off and decoder held fixed.
- T3: transfer to new relevant contexts, and no material regression of protected old skills.
- T4: selected candidate axon/chemical/structural state adds information beyond background activity, viability and age-matched changes.
- T5: for a physical Root or Crystal claim, independent causal attribution and separate reader/recipient controls.

A positive T1 never proves T2–T5. A failed cue can indicate access or readout failure without erasure. [CE CRYSTAL MATH R03](../../../research/full-markdown/R03_03_Formal_Models_and_Reconciliation_1_.md).

## 6. Chemical, glial and tissue-interaction study boundaries

A candidate molecule can alter support, receptor state, axon morphology, bulk activity or learning; these are different measured endpoints. **For this module:** neuromodulatory and astrocytic signaling as observation candidates only.

Before any physical molecular change, a qualified investigator specifies actual species/proteoform, tissue preparation and receptor competence, chosen instrument/assay, appropriate negative/positive controls, target engagement, general health effects and protected old skills. An intervention that increases firing or cell number does not automatically increase Understanding. [BIO-04](../../../processes/BIO-04-sustibus-chemibus.md), [BIO-09](../../../processes/BIO-09-growth-control.md).

## 7. Relative temporal and spatial models

Define early response, delayed functional availability, imaging/biochemical assay measurement times, sample location and viable-condition history independently. Avoid arbitrary "rest = sleep", "stillness = crystal", "lactate rise = new Root" or "MMP9 cleavage = a sentence". A qualitative slow-state model is allowed as a test proposal only; a numerical predictive law requires actual parameter measurements and independent fit.

## 8. Experimental logic and negative controls

| Arm / comparator | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Ordinary qualified preparation or digital-only baseline | estimate maturation/external computation contribution |
| Condition with contingent teaching | candidate acquisition |
| Same information with randomized/yoked ordering (as appropriate) | distinguish contingency from exposure |
| Independent delayed probe | storage/access and functional retention |
| Alternative codebook/access route | discriminate some retrieval failures from loss |
| Calibration/readout control | limit sensor drift and adaptive-decoder explanation |
| Host-only/recipient-only/joined | determine assistance versus actual independent learning |
| Protected skills after proposed change | determine collateral loss and noninferiority |

**Specific falsifier or ambiguity for CE-A21:** reward-hacking, emotional manipulation or clinical claim from unvalidated signals.

Tests that cannot separate target mechanism from a simpler explanation return **UNRESOLVED**.

## 9. Measurement ontology and statistics

Evidence is joined by source/preparation/region/clock/assay, not by one global neural-activity score. A preparation, rather than its thousands of cells/electrodes, is the relevant replicate when comparing independent biological systems. Report raw and derived signals, per-class confusion matrix, validity exclusions, sample attrition, control groups, independent unit count, uncertainty, effect size and negative findings. The formal companion audit corrects the original logistic, mass-balance and retention-model interpretation [R03](../../../research/full-markdown/R03_03_Formal_Models_and_Reconciliation_1_.md).

## 10. Growth or structural-remodeling proposal

**This module's condition:** no neurochemical mood recipe or tissue manipulation from engagement score.

First ask whether more capacity is truly needed; then distinguish *new cells*, *axon/neurite remodeling*, *synaptic strength*, *glial support*, *digital specialization* and *readout access*. A physical change requires separate source/care/maturity qualification and a justified mechanism. It cannot be derived from an AI persona's agreement or a held-out score alone.

## 12. Conclusion, acceptance and release

**Proposed success:** transparent user-controlled support and evidence of educational benefit. **Failure/alternative:** reward-hacking, emotional manipulation or clinical claim from unvalidated signals. A valid negative outcome narrows the tested mechanism and still belongs in the project evidence atlas.

Record scientific claim type (F finding, X synthesis, H hypothesis, D design, A analogy), dated source, research question, independent comparator, 1° endpoint, model/units, confidence and signed decision. Avoid upgrading H to F merely because source terms correspond.

# 1. What exactly is being proposed?

Emotional support is a proposed system role. Neurochemical reward states and living subjective emotion are not established by the presented device.

ChristEye roles are scoped to the carrier and scale. The Soil is the living organoid where this project chooses that embodiment; Sea is the exchange surroundings. Lotus can name host scheduling or local biological coordination only with distinct role assignments; biological Heart is response formation, not independent physical limits. A digital CrystalPack is a reproducible archive, whereas a physical Crystal is a currently unverified content-bearing carrier hypothesis.

# 2. Formal model, operational quantities and limitations

Primary analysis expression / decision rule:

Policy utility = user-rated helpfulness − λ interrupt_cost − μ unwanted_personalization; never optimize a biological wellbeing proxy absent valid measures.

An equation is not an observation. Before estimating any quantity, fix its unit of analysis, data source, clock, denominator, uncertainty method and applicability domain. In particular, true physiological state, task performance, network activity and digitally computed scores are different observables.

Mathematical audit requirements from R03: check dimensions; do not label current-squared exposure as energy without voltage; do not infer causality from correlation; do not treat a logistic maintenance model as automatically bistable; do not clamp chance-corrected retention ratios; do not infer saved content from a concentration or image. Scalar controller objectives need declared weights and separate hard constraints.

# 3. Research question, study design and primary endpoint

Research / engineering contrast: Within-subject randomized presentation comparison with opt-in satisfaction measures and task-equivalent difficulty; analyze false personalization and unwanted interruption.

Comparator and negative-control design: Neutral baseline, nonpersonalized support, opt-out and correction tests; no inference of subjective biological pleasure or distress.

Primary unit: a complete independent digital run/replicate for software studies, or one independently prepared biological system for living experiments. Electrode channels, frames, observations and trials nested inside a preparation are not independent biological replicates. Split at the project, episode, source/preparation and time boundary needed to eliminate leakage.

# 4. Measurement and data collection contract

| **Measured dimension**  | **Recording requirement**                                                                    |
|-------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Input                   | Preserve raw or claim-adequate source, versioned encoding, intended recipient and permission |
| Exposure / training     | Actual delivered events, timestamp, ordering, rest/probe history, unmatched events           |

| Biological observations | Only lab-qualified measurement, location, source/lot, validity, calibration and time         |

# 5. Expected discriminations and alternatives

A result in the favorable direction supports only the scoped claim if the comparator and measurement contract pass. It does not promote adjacent claims about subjective experience, complete semantic understanding, exact neural storage address, physical Crystal packing or transfer.

- Behavioral labels may anthropomorphize tissue

- Reward can be misassigned

- Over-personalization exposes private data

For this module the priority alternative explanations and interpretation checks are: Neutral baseline, nonpersonalized support, opt-out and correction tests; no inference of subjective biological pleasure or distress. Re-check support and interface validity before naming a Root failure. A negative result restricts the tested preparation, interval and task; it cannot be rewritten as success through a new verbal definition.

# 6. Engineering acceptance and falsification plan

- No claim that an organoid wants food or experiences emotions

- Essential Milk remains independent of task success

- Supportive policy cannot manipulate or coerce the user

Independent outcome metrics: User-rated helpfulness, consent compliance, task improvement, stability/alert indicators, feedback discrimination, burden.

Promote from H (hypothesis) to a scoped F (finding) only after a dated measurement or citable external study with matching preparation and claim. Use D for declared engineering choice, X for cross-study synthesis and A for metaphor. Preregister the primary endpoint, eligibility, comparisons, precision/power assumptions, exclusions, failure conditions and label of UNRESOLVED.

# 7. Handoff for development and research

Software engineering: Implement consented preference and feedback service. Add policy-level evaluation with negative cases. Keep tissue health metrics separate from affective labels.

Living research: supply a qualified institutional preparation, traceable source/consent, explicit observation coverage, frozen task and evaluator, and independent laboratory signoff. If a physical field or limit is unknown, leave it null and block that experiment; do not borrow numerical settings from a different preparation.
