# 1. What exactly is being proposed?

An observer trained on encoded telemetry is not a chemically sensing cell without established receptor and validated transduction.

ChristEye roles are scoped to the carrier and scale. The Soil is the living organoid where this project chooses that embodiment; Sea is the exchange surroundings. Lotus can name host scheduling or local biological coordination only with distinct role assignments; biological Heart is response formation, not independent physical limits. A digital CrystalPack is a reproducible archive, whereas a physical Crystal is a currently unverified content-bearing carrier hypothesis.

# 2. Formal model, operational quantities and limitations

Primary analysis expression / decision rule:

Lead=t_outcome−t_alert \>0; alert_precision=TP/(TP+FP); performance must beat matched digital and untrained baselines for the declared claim.

An equation is not an observation. Before estimating any quantity, fix its unit of analysis, data source, clock, denominator, uncertainty method and applicability domain. In particular, true physiological state, task performance, network activity and digitally computed scores are different observables.

Mathematical audit requirements from R03: check dimensions; do not label current-squared exposure as energy without voltage; do not infer causality from correlation; do not treat a logistic maintenance model as automatically bistable; do not clamp chance-corrected retention ratios; do not infer saved content from a concentration or image. Scalar controller objectives need declared weights and separate hard constraints.

# 3. Research question, study design and primary endpoint

Research / engineering contrast: CE-DEV E04 O1, E05 O2, E06 adaptive support; CE-CRYSTAL-SYS EX09. Freeze cutoff and compare independent episodes plus later preparations.

Comparator and negative-control design: Digital classifier, shuffled labels, untrained O, fixed schedule, route faults and missingness, independent evaluator.

Primary unit: a complete independent digital run/replicate for software studies, or one independently prepared biological system for living experiments. Electrode channels, frames, observations and trials nested inside a preparation are not independent biological replicates. Split at the project, episode, source/preparation and time boundary needed to eliminate leakage.

# 5. Expected discriminations and alternatives

A result in the favorable direction supports only the scoped claim if the comparator and measurement contract pass. It does not promote adjacent claims about subjective experience, complete semantic understanding, exact neural storage address, physical Crystal packing or transfer.

- Observer may learn service timestamps rather than need

- Correlated feature does not establish cause

- Large signal is not necessarily distress

For this module the priority alternative explanations and interpretation checks are: Digital classifier, shuffled labels, untrained O, fixed schedule, route faults and missingness, independent evaluator. Re-check support and interface validity before naming a Root failure. A negative result restricts the tested preparation, interval and task; it cannot be rewritten as success through a new verbal definition.

# 6. Engineering acceptance and falsification plan

- No post-outcome feature leakage

- UNKNOWN when sensors stale

- No direct access to pump, stimulation or genetic control

Independent outcome metrics: Macro recall, precision-recall area, warning lead time, false alerts/hour, availability, downstream support contribution.

Promote from H (hypothesis) to a scoped F (finding) only after a dated measurement or citable external study with matching preparation and claim. Use D for declared engineering choice, X for cross-study synthesis and A for metaphor. Preregister the primary endpoint, eligibility, comparisons, precision/power assumptions, exclusions, failure conditions and label of UNRESOLVED.

# 7. Handoff for development and research

Software engineering: Implement digital O1/O2 benchmark with leakage tests. Add shadow routing and alert dashboard. Write independent living-qualification study design.

Living research: supply a qualified institutional preparation, traceable source/consent, explicit observation coverage, frozen task and evaluator, and independent laboratory signoff. If a physical field or limit is unknown, leave it null and block that experiment; do not borrow numerical settings from a different preparation.
