# CE-A02 — Long Term Storage · Scientific companion

**Revision 3.1** · [Engineering design](../design/CE-A02_long_term_storage_Design.md) · [Evidence ledger](../../../research/evidence/anaiero-biology-2026-11.md)

## 1. Testable claim at the correct physical scale

**Proposed ordinary substrate:** proposed spinal Root tissue distinct from verified digital CAS storage.

**Module capability to explain:** qualify learning → delayed readout without teaching → alternate cue access → protected-task test → mechanism study.

For a living claim, define a particular preparation, task relation, independent receiving/teacher channels, readout, delayed evaluation horizon and result. The original engineering roles **do not** declare that a named molecule *is* the Word, Heart, Honey, Soul, Root or Lotus; these are testable role assignments with measured physical participants.

## 2. Source-backed candidate participants

**Unique candidates for CE-A02:** KIBRA/PKMζ, DLG4/SHANK/HOMER, BDNF/TrkB, astrocyte MCT assay candidates.

| Mechanistic class | Scientific interpretation to test | Minimum discriminator |
|---|---|---|
| Cellular identity | actual sourced neuron/glia/progenitor subtypes in this preparation, not generic "brain cells" | multi-marker identity, source batch and region |
| Axonal architecture | trace soma-axon terminals, synaptic candidate structures and regional persistence | registered morphology AND a separate functional influence test |
| Receptor/signaling | ordinary candidate forms named above, within the receptor/compartment where reported | distinguish effect from generalized excitability |
| Structural maintenance | scaffold, astrocyte and ECM state if implicated by the candidate hypothesis | delayed relation-specific effect with controlled viability |
| Nutrient/exchange | source-qualified substrate and selected transport metabolites; care remains task independent | calibration and supply/uptake/dilution alternatives |
| Digital storage/replay | source-hashed episodes and immutable model/filesystem state | independent software restoration, not biological engram claim |

Relevant literature: [2026 organoid readouts B01](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44222-026-00507-6), [2026 corticospinal connectoid B02](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42201810/), [2025 sensory assembloid B03](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08808-3), [2011 lactate-memory study B07](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3073831/). None asserts the project has reproduced those results.

## 3. Axon and spinal Root hypotheses

Treat **Root tissue** as a candidate spinal-derived system and **learned Root** as a delayed retained relation. They are linked by an explicit evidence edge only after morphological, functional and task-specific comparisons; never by the metaphor "spine" alone. Axonal projections are neuronal processes; morphological continuity is not equivalent to directed transmission or retained knowledge. [BIO-02](../../../processes/BIO-02-axon-spinal-root.md).

**Study-specific axon question:** trace soma-axon terminals, synaptic candidate structures and regional persistence. Record source identity, target identity, imaging coverage, timing and field/electrode artifacts, preparation uncertainty, competing common-drive routes and reproducibility across truly independent biological units.

## 4. Cells, Crest and identity

The Crest is an audited cell-source and maturation service; production requires qualified human SOP and source consent. Proposed cell tracking uses source_lot_id, clone_lineage_id, per-observation cell_instance_id and track_id/confidence. **Clone identity does not preserve learned state.** A heritable barcode can identify a shared ancestry; collision/dropout and perturbation make it unsuitable as a presumed unique baby-cell ID. [CellTag-multi (2024)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-023-01931-4), [lineage tracing limits (2025)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11996212/).

## 5. Acquisition, Root retention and competing causes

**Candidate staged test:** qualify learning → delayed readout without teaching → alternate cue access → protected-task test → mechanism study.

Predeclare:
- T1: prospective improvement under declared contingent teaching compared with matched noncontingent input and source maturation.
- T2: delayed selective performance with all answer-bearing teaching routes off and decoder held fixed.
- T3: transfer to new relevant contexts, and no material regression of protected old skills.
- T4: selected candidate axon/chemical/structural state adds information beyond background activity, viability and age-matched changes.
- T5: for a physical Root or Crystal claim, independent causal attribution and separate reader/recipient controls.

A positive T1 never proves T2–T5. A failed cue can indicate access or readout failure without erasure. [CE CRYSTAL MATH R03](../../../research/full-markdown/R03_03_Formal_Models_and_Reconciliation_1_.md).

## 6. Chemical, glial and tissue-interaction study boundaries

A candidate molecule can alter support, receptor state, axon morphology, bulk activity or learning; these are different measured endpoints. **For this module:** KIBRA/PKMζ, DLG4/SHANK/HOMER, BDNF/TrkB, astrocyte MCT assay candidates.

Before any physical molecular change, a qualified investigator specifies actual species/proteoform, tissue preparation and receptor competence, chosen instrument/assay, appropriate negative/positive controls, target engagement, general health effects and protected old skills. An intervention that increases firing or cell number does not automatically increase Understanding. [BIO-04](../../../processes/BIO-04-sustibus-chemibus.md), [BIO-09](../../../processes/BIO-09-growth-control.md).

## 7. Relative temporal and spatial models

Define early response, delayed functional availability, imaging/biochemical assay measurement times, sample location and viable-condition history independently. Avoid arbitrary "rest = sleep", "stillness = crystal", "lactate rise = new Root" or "MMP9 cleavage = a sentence". A qualitative slow-state model is allowed as a test proposal only; a numerical predictive law requires actual parameter measurements and independent fit.

## 8. Experimental logic and negative controls

| Arm / comparator | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Ordinary qualified preparation or digital-only baseline | estimate maturation/external computation contribution |
| Condition with contingent teaching | candidate acquisition |
| Same information with randomized/yoked ordering (as appropriate) | distinguish contingency from exposure |
| Independent delayed probe | storage/access and functional retention |
| Alternative codebook/access route | discriminate some retrieval failures from loss |
| Calibration/readout control | limit sensor drift and adaptive-decoder explanation |
| Host-only/recipient-only/joined | determine assistance versus actual independent learning |
| Protected skills after proposed change | determine collateral loss and noninferiority |

**Specific falsifier or ambiguity for CE-A02:** corrupted retrieval or generalized excitability labeled physical memory.

Tests that cannot separate target mechanism from a simpler explanation return **UNRESOLVED**.

## 9. Measurement ontology and statistics

Evidence is joined by source/preparation/region/clock/assay, not by one global neural-activity score. A preparation, rather than its thousands of cells/electrodes, is the relevant replicate when comparing independent biological systems. Report raw and derived signals, per-class confusion matrix, validity exclusions, sample attrition, control groups, independent unit count, uncertainty, effect size and negative findings. The formal companion audit corrects the original logistic, mass-balance and retention-model interpretation [R03](../../../research/full-markdown/R03_03_Formal_Models_and_Reconciliation_1_.md).

## 10. Growth or structural-remodeling proposal

**This module's condition:** propose regional renewal only after a separate scientific review; no automatic cell supply.

First ask whether more capacity is truly needed; then distinguish *new cells*, *axon/neurite remodeling*, *synaptic strength*, *glial support*, *digital specialization* and *readout access*. A physical change requires separate source/care/maturity qualification and a justified mechanism. It cannot be derived from an AI persona's agreement or a held-out score alone.

## 12. Conclusion, acceptance and release

**Proposed success:** delayed content-specific Root function and independently restored digital storage. **Failure/alternative:** corrupted retrieval or generalized excitability labeled physical memory. A valid negative outcome narrows the tested mechanism and still belongs in the project evidence atlas.

## 15. A02-specific science audit and quantitative falsification packet

### 15.1. Why digital archival and biological retention differ

The ability to retrieve hashed file bytes on a new host is a test of *data integrity*. A biological population that responds differently because of its history is a candidate for *learning*. Preserved response under controlled delay demonstrates *bounded retention*. A physical structure retaining a specific relation is a further causal carrier hypothesis. A transferable knowledge Crystal is a further claim beyond all those.

The project names spinal-derived matter as a physical Root, but the documented literature does not establish that such a spinal organoid is the location for arbitrary semantic long-term memory. Corticospinal connectoid research demonstrates axon growth and functional communication in a specific human in-vitro model [Gibbons et al. 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42201810/), not a general memory storage device.

### 15.2. Mechanistic candidate table with actual inferential limits

| Hypothesis | Named ordinary participants | Literature support | Negative or alternative reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task-linked synaptic maintenance | KIBRA/PKMζ | [Science Advances 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11204205/), specific animal models | a general viability/kinase effect or different maintenance mechanism |
| Local receiving structure | DLG4/PSD95, SynGAP, SHANK/HOMER | [Cell 2016 PSD assembly](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5564291/) | concentration/physical scaffold with no content discrimination |
| Astrocytic metabolic support | MCT1/MCT4 (astrocytic), MCT2 (neuronal) | [Cell 2011 rat learning](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3073831/) | metabolism correlates with activity, not a specific stored fact |
| History-sensitive receiving state | verified BDNF proteoforms/TrkB and receiver partners | [Science Advances 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40991708/) | generic receptor activation, not a portable Word or peptide-based installed curriculum |
| Spinal axon organization | qualified cortical/spinal source, axon projections, terminal evidence | [Cell Reports 2026 connectoid](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42201810/) | shared drive, bulk excitability and incidental contact |
| Biological delayed task | independent organoid preparation with controlled task | [Cell Reports 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41720084/) | tested short-lasting adaptation rather than durable general learning |

**Scientist's rule:** a positive molecular assay cannot replace a retained-task probe; a retained-task probe cannot by itself identify a molecular carrier.

### 15.3. Models and unit checks

For a valid 3-class task (X, Y, HOLD) with support in each class, macro recall is the arithmetic mean of the *three per-class recall values*. If \(n_c\) is the number of valid trials in class \(c\), \(r_c = TP_c/n_c\), and \(M=(r_X+r_Y+r_{HOLD})/3\). Separately report missing/invalid trial rate, task-class balance and preparation-level uncertainty. This metric is distinct from raw accuracy; a constant HOLD strategy can have deceptively high accuracy when HOLD is common.

A chance-corrected ratio may be described by \(R=(P_{delayed}-P_{chance})/(P_{immediate}-P_{chance})\) only when the immediate numerator is meaningfully above the chance comparator. It may exceed 1 or be negative, and cannot be clamped without distorting evidence. Report absolute immediate/delayed scores first. [R03 corrected formal models](../../../research/full-markdown/R03_03_Formal_Models_and_Reconciliation_1_.md).

The access hypothesis \(P_{observed}=P_{chance}+(K-P_{chance})A\) contains two latent variables, retained capacity \(K\) and momentary access \(A\). A single observed score cannot distinguish them. Alternative prior cues, source/decoder calibration, matched support and no re-teaching supply additional discriminating evidence, not certain anatomical localization.

### 15.4. Assay constraints and false-positive avoidance

A molecular study must declare **what is physically measured** (cell/region/molecule/compartment, actual assay, time/location/units) and whether the molecular readout is repeated or destructive. RNA dynamics require special observation and cannot continuously inventory every living cell; optical coverage and handling matter. Neurite extension and dendritic spine change are not birth events. Use separate source, tracer and time/geometry records [2025 long-term morphodynamics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09151-3).

An "A02-spinal Root" claim requires both relevant spinal identity and evidence of a learned relation **attributable to that tissue**, beyond an adapted external decoder. A tissue might participate in a circuit without uniquely storing the relation.

### 15.5. Falsification decisions

1. **A02-D digital restore fails:** reject digital durability regardless of the biological experiment.
2. **Biological task improves only with adaptive host/decoder:** classify **hybrid computational improvement**, not changed tissue.
3. **Delayed task returns to control after matched interval:** bounded negative result for retention in the preparation and test horizon.
4. **Structural candidate increases but function does not:** reject the claim that the measured expansion establishes new Understanding.
5. **Biological candidate affects general health and all tasks equally:** no content-specific engram conclusion.
6. **B improves only while joined:** report assisted use; do not infer independent recipient acquisition.
7. **All five gates for portable carrier unpassed:** retain physical Crystal as a research hypothesis, not an installable file or peptide.

### 15.6. Human governance and next experiments

A scientific director defines one *primary* learned relation and predeclares a delayed endpoint with justified biological sample size. The cell foundry qualifies source line and spinal identity, electrophysiologist validates actual events, analyst maintains correct independent replicate units, and limit controller prevents any unreviewed care changes. Biological interventions require separately documented molecular form/target, qualified preparation SOP and oversight; no unpublished "ideal peptide feeding" algorithm can be inferred from current results.

# 1. What exactly is being proposed?

Neural consolidation and digital data durability are different forms of persistence. Delayed biological function is a separate empirical endpoint.

ChristEye roles are scoped to the carrier and scale. The Soil is the living organoid where this project chooses that embodiment; Sea is the exchange surroundings. Lotus can name host scheduling or local biological coordination only with distinct role assignments; biological Heart is response formation, not independent physical limits. A digital CrystalPack is a reproducible archive, whereas a physical Crystal is a currently unverified content-bearing carrier hypothesis.

# 2. Formal model, operational quantities and limitations

Primary analysis expression / decision rule:

Storage integrity = 1 − corrupted_verified_objects / audited_objects; RPO = latest durable event gap; RTO = measured fresh-node restoration elapsed time.

An equation is not an observation. Before estimating any quantity, fix its unit of analysis, data source, clock, denominator, uncertainty method and applicability domain. In particular, true physiological state, task performance, network activity and digitally computed scores are different observables.

Mathematical audit requirements from R03: check dimensions; do not label current-squared exposure as energy without voltage; do not infer causality from correlation; do not treat a logistic maintenance model as automatically bistable; do not clamp chance-corrected retention ratios; do not infer saved content from a concentration or image. Scalar controller objectives need declared weights and separate hard constraints.

# 3. Research question, study design and primary endpoint

Research / engineering contrast: Fault-inject interruption before rename, after rename but before metadata commit, and during index rebuild. Verify that no committed path points to missing bytes and that recovery replays the outbox exactly once logically.

Comparator and negative-control design: Cold restore from separate host; hash corruption; permissions migration; old-index query after deletion; retained biological records explicitly marked telemetry, not an engram backup.

Primary unit: a complete independent digital run/replicate for software studies, or one independently prepared biological system for living experiments. Electrode channels, frames, observations and trials nested inside a preparation are not independent biological replicates. Split at the project, episode, source/preparation and time boundary needed to eliminate leakage.

# 5. Expected discriminations and alternatives

A result in the favorable direction supports only the scoped claim if the comparator and measurement contract pass. It does not promote adjacent claims about subjective experience, complete semantic understanding, exact neural storage address, physical Crystal packing or transfer.

- A storage snapshot is not a neural engram backup

- Index corruption may appear as forgetting

- Repetition may teach the evaluator

For this module the priority alternative explanations and interpretation checks are: Cold restore from separate host; hash corruption; permissions migration; old-index query after deletion; retained biological records explicitly marked telemetry, not an engram backup. Re-check support and interface validity before naming a Root failure. A negative result restricts the tested preparation, interval and task; it cannot be rewritten as success through a new verbal definition.

# 6. Engineering acceptance and falsification plan

- Reboot and restore preserve source-addressed objects

- Conflicting sources remain distinguishable

- A failed retention probe blocks a biological-memory claim

Independent outcome metrics: Bit-integrity error rate, retrieval accuracy, stale-result rate, snapshot recovery time, topic-shift performance, old-skill noninferiority.

Promote from H (hypothesis) to a scoped F (finding) only after a dated measurement or citable external study with matching preparation and claim. Use D for declared engineering choice, X for cross-study synthesis and A for metaphor. Preregister the primary endpoint, eligibility, comparisons, precision/power assumptions, exclusions, failure conditions and label of UNRESOLVED.

# 7. Handoff for development and research

Software engineering: Implement content-addressed ledger. Version indexes and snapshot recovery test. Compare immediate vs delayed held-out retrieval.

Living research: supply a qualified institutional preparation, traceable source/consent, explicit observation coverage, frozen task and evaluator, and independent laboratory signoff. If a physical field or limit is unknown, leave it null and block that experiment; do not borrow numerical settings from a different preparation.
