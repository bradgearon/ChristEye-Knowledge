# 1. Engineering purpose and boundary

Anaiero is a developmental host and typed StarChip architecture. Software modules can receive authorized experiences, organize their knowledge, measure durable skills, compile digital CrystalPacks, replay them into compatible learners and export reproducible functional artifacts. The living embodiment is a separate research adapter to independently supported neural preparations. As supplied, no complete mind, neural knowledge packing, peptide-coded instruction, or transferable biological Crystal has been demonstrated.

This release replaces the short v2 index-style documents. The source research has been preserved as original files under research/source_documents and corrected by an explicit equation audit. The 34 legacy v1 StarChip DOCX index specifications and generated-document tooling are deliberately not carried forward; their interface concept survives in the new registry and module records.

User / approved connector

-\> A19 quarantine + provenance

-\> A01 episode ledger -\> A02 durable content store -\> A20 filesystem snapshot

-\> A07 curriculum / A16 Roots / A06 retention evaluator

-\> A12 Lotus (planning) -\> A22 limits (veto)

-\> A24 observer -\> E sealed evaluator -\> provenance feedback

-\> A10 compile Crystal -\> A13 digest clone -\> A14 staged replay -\> Root retest

A digital Crystal is data plus reader, replay and assessment contracts. The biological candidate P_crystal is a material-carrier research claim with content and reader requirements; it does not share an installer with the digital pack. A physical action is never inferred from a digital provision or a model-generated suggestion.

# 4. System-of-record rules

- An immutable UsageEvent links actor, source, rights, time, content hash and causal history. Deleted/revoked source cannot enter new exports.

- The ContentStore owns bytes; metadata and indexes are derived. Each logical path resolves through a snapshot to a content digest.

- RootRecord stores independently measured competence and delayed tests. Never infer a Root merely from an image, a molecular concentration or one correct response.

- WindEnvelope carries source, recipient, version, permission, payload digest, time and validity. Requested and actual biological delivery remain separate records.

- LotusDecision proposes an action with alternatives, predicted benefit and uncertainty. Independent L authorizes or vetoes; E holds sealed outcomes.

- CrystalManifest is immutable and names assets, rights, compatibility, test results and installability. Research-only physical candidate records have installable=false.

# 6. Key formulas and numerical claims

Primary class score: MAR=(Recall_X+Recall_Y+Recall_HOLD)/3. Proposed predecessor targets (NOT achieved): acquisition ≥0.80 MAR, 24 h ≥0.75 absolute performance, 7 d ≥0.70, protected old-task loss no worse than 0.05 under an appropriate noninferiority analysis. Earlier studies reported no persistent advantage after 45 min in one mouse cortical-organoid cartpole preparation (S02).

Chance-corrected retention R_U(τ)=(P_τ−P_chance)/(P_0−P_chance) only when immediate performance exceeds chance sufficiently for an interpretable denominator; R_U may exceed 1 or become negative. Report absolute scores and confidence intervals regardless. Digital memory durability is tested by cryptographic restoration and independent skill tests, not this biological equation.

Illustrative raw acquisition throughput (not confirmed instrument purchase): 1,020 channels × 20,000 samples/s × 2 bytes = 40.8 MB/s; 146.88 GB/hour; 3.52512 TB/day. Include optical streams and system overhead separately. Energy = ∫V(t)I(t)dt, not merely ∫I(t)²dt; use the whole-system boundary.

# 7. Biological function/claim separation

| **Claim**          | **Necessary evidence; what it does NOT establish**                                                               |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Interface          | Stimulus/response genuine with artifacts excluded; not task learning.                                            |
| Acquisition        | Matched contingent feedback improves held-out response; not delayed memory.                                      |
| Roots              | Delayed selective performance without answer-bearing teacher; not exact molecular location.                      |
| Astrocyte role     | Independent prediction and eventually selective causal contribution; not universally astrocyte-stored semantics. |
| Joining            | Measured electronic/physical route contribution; not memory copy.                                                |
| Physical Crystal   | Bounded carrier correlated and selectively linked to learned relation with reader contract; not yet portable.    |
| Remanifestation    | Recipient-only competence after teaching routes and cached answers removed; not identity of engram.              |
| Biological support | Qualified lab observations; not recipe, food pill or peptide software update.                                    |

# 8. Research audit precedence

The supplied research edition 03 corrects several false or overly broad older claims: logistic maintenance is not bistability; removal of a well-mixed fluid does not necessarily concentrate solute; a quiet culture is not sleep; neural activity displacement does not prove learning; the MMP-9/TIMP-1 abundance ratio is not a local activity readout; a softmax score is not calibrated confidence; and a physical Crystal cannot be inferred from stable performance or a condensed protein image. These corrections govern all 24 companions.
