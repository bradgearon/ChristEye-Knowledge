## Intended public materials

Public-safe design documents, research proposals, experimental methodology summaries, software prototypes, non-sensitive scientific data, validation results, architecture descriptions and milestone history may be published under the WDS license, subject to provenance and third-party rights.

## Withheld material

- Patent applications, patent drafts, patent claim language, and confidential disclosures from those documents.
- Raw personal or private conversation/application logs, credentials, donor information, and other sensitive information. Curated experiment or decision logs may be published after review.
- Any third-party source whose rights do not permit redistribution.

A mention of an existing patent in a bibliographic or provenance record does not authorize publication of that patent's contents. Before publishing, the project owner should review technical documents for patent-specific confidential descriptions or claims. This staging release does not establish legal patent protection or certify clearance.

## Scientific status

Use **Finding**, **Synthesis**, **Hypothesis**, **Design Decision**, and **Analogy** labels where applicable. A working simulation is not evidence of a biological achievement. Experimental records remain intellectual property of their respective lawful owners; this license grants only the permissions expressly stated.

## Publishing

The distribution archive is a prepared package, **not an actual commit or a live website**. It was staged after the GitHub integration rejected write operations with HTTP 403. Do not publish it until a human owner has reviewed the withheld-material screen.
