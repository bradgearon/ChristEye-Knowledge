# Understanding the layers of Anaiero

A modular system is only useful if its parts have clear responsibilities.

**Lotus** coordinates authorized software learning and maintenance choices. **WindBus** delivers typed, source-attributed messages between modules. **Roots** are relationships that have been tested for durable use. A **digital Crystal** packages a verifiable collection of sources, curricula, software state, and replay instructions.

In a separate biological research track, **Soil** is the proposed living organoid as a whole. Axons and dendrites are parts of neurons, and a proposed spinal-derived Root system needs its own source identity and functional evidence. A digital copy of a Crystal does not clone a biological learned state.

### Support is not reward

**Milk** names essential physiological support when we are discussing living preparations. It must not depend on whether a learner answers correctly. **Honey** names selective learning feedback. **Bread** and **Meat** describe foundational versus advanced curricula when used in the digital education system. **ChemiBus** holds molecular observations, while **SustiBus** holds qualified care status.

Those separations make it possible to ask real questions. Did the digital index recover? Did the learner acquire a new capability? Was a molecular change observed? Did an axonal route change? These are different measurements.

### The evidence boundary

The existing public Python modules demonstrate selected digital behavior. Experimental organoid learning, molecular transfer, and a portable physical memory Crystal remain independent research questions. Our documentation identifies the distinction throughout the project.
