# Design intent and release boundary

Design ID CE CRYSTAL SYS 001, revision 1.0, 15 November 2026. This specification extends CE DEV 001 revision 0.4 and preserves its module separation, independent support, fixed-readout comparisons and staged joining. It adds a research path from the inventor's organoid Soil to a bounded physical Crystal candidate. The two projects share interfaces and evidence rules; they do not yet share a demonstrated packing process.

The current release is a complete research architecture and documentation package. It contains no qualified tissue-production SOP, selected stimulation envelope, fabricated carrier or completed biological experiment. Those missing inputs have explicit owners and closure evidence below. Existing preparation methods must be selected for the actual cell source and instrument. No concentration, exposure, timing or production method is inferred from a ChristEye name.

The principal deliverable to seek is a living core that retains a declared context-sensitive relation after teaching ends. The next is a bounded material organization linked to that retention. Useful joining, independent recipient acquisition and practical computing advantage have separate acceptance decisions.

C sends observations through H to O. O produces an advisory status. S may later propose an action. H chooses only among allowed actions and L grants or rejects the physical permit. E receives closed trial records and supplies teaching outcomes only in training. During evaluation, E does not release answer-bearing feedback. P_crystal connects to a host only through a declared and measured interface.

The living Heart is C's or B's response-forming organization. L is an external protective mechanism, not a replacement definition of Heart. Lotus can describe local coordination in tissue and scheduling in H if separate role records specify each mechanism. The software archive retains code, curriculum and evidence; call it the reproducibility archive rather than equating it with P_crystal.

## Physical partitions and preparation versions

Keep dry electronics, the culture compartment and the service compartment distinct. Initial modules have independently identified support paths. Shared liquid creates an additional route and changes the interpretation of an electronic disconnect. The first design uses a supported recording platform, its unmodified chamber, a registered optical approach where needed, and verified synchronization. A custom carrier or fluid path requires fit, material, delivery and containment qualification before biological interpretation.

CE DEV 001 begins with a planar neuronal and astrocytic culture. That remains a practical commissioning branch. The organoid branch has its own source, geometry, deep measurement coverage, exchange limits and qualification record. Data from a planar culture may guide an organoid study but cannot pass its release gates. Regional and species identities remain in every record. The main organoid and a connected human brain are not interchangeable targets; in vivo or human integration is outside this instrument release.

The v0.4 targets are retained as proposed project targets: core acquisition 0.80 macro recall, absolute delayed performance 0.75 at 24 hours and 0.70 at seven days, no more than 0.05 loss on protected capability, and joined improvement of 0.05 or a predefined resource benefit at noninferior quality. They are inherited targets, not measured performance or universal biological thresholds. Final confirmation criteria must specify uncertainty and be frozen before confirmation. A failure at seven days does not erase a demonstrated shorter horizon; the claim must name its horizon.

# Reference task and information accounting

Use the existing context-and-order task so the new research connects directly to the living computer. Context C0 or C1 precedes a two-token sequence and a GO event. The task codebook determines physical input profiles only after those profiles are qualified.

| **Context**                      | **Sequence** | **Required action**         |
|----------------------------------|--------------|-----------------------------|
| C0                               | AB           | X                           |
| C0                               | BA           | Y                           |
| C1                               | AB           | Y                           |
| C1                               | BA           | X                           |
| C0 or C1                         | AA or BB     | HOLD                        |
| Missing context or unknown token | Any          | UNRESOLVED interface status |

There are eight valid context-sequence combinations. HOLD occupies four, while X and Y each occupy two. A constant HOLD output can obtain 0.50 raw accuracy; its macro recall is one third. Report the confusion matrix, every context-sequence cell and macro recall. Include context-blind, last-token, token-count and exact-rule software baselines. The exact rule engine verifies the task; biological learning does not require beating that oracle.

UNRESOLVED is initially an instrument status. Biological abstention would require its own trained response class and evaluation. INVALID means the recording or delivery cannot support a decision; it is never relabeled a correct HOLD. Service availability uses all scheduled opportunities as its denominator, while valid-trial performance is reported separately.

Freeze the input map, permitted features, artifact exclusion, decoder, normalization and decision window before the confirmatory comparison. When an adaptive digital decoder is studied, label the result hybrid reservoir learning. Do not mix it with evidence for changed tissue under a fixed reader. Split at the level of independent preparations and source episodes; adjacent recording windows cannot appear on both sides of a held-out split.

Every bridge has an information budget as well as an exposure budget. Record whether its message contains a cue, answer, confidence, status, teaching outcome or support proposal. Replaying a teacher's correct answers can teach the recipient; that is a valid control for teaching, not an information-free placebo. Matching total charge does not match temporal structure, spatial coverage, task information or metabolic consequences.

# Lifecycle and checkpoints

The research cycle is characterization, acquisition, intervening activity and support, candidate stabilization, delayed selective use, and possible revision. It is a lifecycle with measurable transitions, not a prescribed Wind-to-silence-to-dry-solid chemistry.

| **State**               | **Allowed work**                                            | **Exit record**                                         |
|-------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| SOURCE and MATURING     | Qualified care and characterization                         | Source, development and identity release                |
| INTERFACE READY         | Bounded cue and recording qualification                     | Input separability, artifact and response record        |
| TRAINING                | Approved cues and declared contingent feedback              | Complete delivered events and closed trial outcomes     |
| INTERVENING             | Assigned quiet, replay or other qualified condition         | Actual internal activity, support and exposure record   |
| CANDIDATE STABILIZATION | One specified mechanism study                               | Target engagement and broad state measurements          |
| EVALUATION              | Frozen reader, no teaching feedback                         | Delayed selective outcomes with validity and provenance |
| REVISION                | Scoped corrective learning under protected-task constraints | Target and collateral effects separately                |
| HOLD or RETIRE          | Qualified care or facility disposition                      | Reason, affected claims and final state                 |

Task pause ends new task stimulation while maintaining the qualified care state. A fluid fault uses its specific response, not a universal command to stop every pump. Host loss must not replay queued events after restart. A candidate cannot act around a limit by changing profile names. Limit definitions are independent of the learner and versioned separately.

# Experimental program

The following cards specify questions, contrasts, endpoints and interpretation. They are study designs, not runnable biological recipes. A qualified laboratory selects assay methods and actual operating parameters for the chosen preparation. The molecular and systems tracks can inform one another, but successful prodomain processing is not a prerequisite for testing all learning mechanisms.

## EX01 Observation and interface validity

Question: can the platform distinguish delivered cues and genuine responses from artifact, missing data and drift? Compare the assembled nonbiological test arrangement, sham events and the qualified living preparation. Measure timing, artifact recovery, usable coverage, input separability and response reliability. Keep physical delivery receipts separate from requested events. A failure is an interface limitation, not evidence that the Soil cannot learn. Exit: a locked codebook and measurement contract usable by EX04. Evidence maps to Eyes, Light, Sinew and Joint.

## EX02 Molecular Word identity

Question: which verified BDNF-related forms arise in the chosen compartment and which are distinguishable by the receiver? Compare identified precursor and processed species with appropriate analytical references. Astrocytic processing is a cellular route, not a fourth protease. Record form, compartment, recovery, purity, receptor context and analytical uncertainty. A shared terminus does not imply identical trafficking or timing; a distinct terminus does not establish different task content. Exit: a scoped molecular inventory and receiver question, whether or not distinct products appear. This addresses the older E1 without presuming its result.

## EX03 Preparatory Word and order effects

Question: does an earlier participant change receptor organization in a way that mediates a later response? Distinguish each participant alone, their combination and the relevant exposure sequences with matched elapsed time and comparable effective exposure. Include vehicle and assay controls and an independently observed receiver-state endpoint. A combination interaction uses a justified reference model; it is not inferred from the arithmetic sum alone. The primary mechanism question is mediation by receptor state, not simply which order gives a larger response. No universal ordering or reagent cost is assumed. An equivalently small order effect narrows this sequence hypothesis without proving that the participants are unrelated.

## EX04 Acquisition and Roots

Question: does teaching history improve later context-and-order discrimination in C under a frozen reader? Compare contingent teaching, a defined exposure-matched noncontingent condition and an untrained or time-matched condition. Record what information each control receives. Primary delayed performance uses the preregistered horizon; immediate performance is secondary evidence for acquisition. Retain the v0.4 candidate checkpoints at 45 minutes, 24 hours and seven days only where support and repeated-probe plans are qualified. These are planned checkpoints, not biological constants. Maturation, artifact changes and digital adaptation are competing explanations. Exit: retained capability at the stated horizon, or a bounded negative result.

## EX05 Intervening activity and Water offices

Question: how do activity, exchange and access between teaching and testing affect later performance? Quiet external input, replay and ongoing stimulation are different experiences; measure actual internal activity, support state and cumulative exposure. Transport, clearance, receptor access and response variability remain separate quantities. A tracer can characterize a selected solute's transport, not all memory-related molecules. The primary contrast is delayed task performance under interpretable conditions. There is no universal neural turbulence index and no requirement that every beneficial interval be silent. Exit: a preparation-specific relationship, including no detectable benefit of quiet.

## EX06 Matrix window and selective stabilization

Question: does a time-dependent matrix process change retention beyond its effects on acquisition, health or cue access? First verify that the candidate matrix and relevant activity exist in this preparation. Compare justified timing conditions using local activity and matrix observations; do not infer permissivity solely from an abundance ratio. Keep acquisition strength, exposure history and observation effects interpretable. The original open-during/closed-after story is one hypothesis among continuous, graded and context-dependent models. Monotonic results constrain the sampled intervention range; they do not disprove all possible timing effects. Exit: a scoped causal or associational result, not automatic Crystal status.

## EX07 Retrieval and maintenance

Question: is a loss of expression better explained by access, ongoing maintenance, measurement failure or lost task-relevant organization? Use alternative previously valid cues and defined health-preserving context changes with teaching off. A return of the selective response supports continued availability somewhere in the tested system; absent recovery leaves several explanations open. Maintenance-pathway studies, including any KIBRA-related investigation, require their own selective target and general-function controls. They cannot be treated as equivalent to draining fluid. Exit: an identified dependence or an explicitly unresolved result. A failed fresh-learning comparison is not a unique assay of erasure.

## EX08 Physical carrier and packing

Question: does a specified material organization contribute to a particular acquired relation? Nominate one candidate class and define its boundary, measurement and reader before searching for favorable images. Compare different teaching histories and matched untrained or activity-matched states. Link structure with delayed function using discovery and confirmation data separately. Generic concentration, stronger firing, matrix density or biochemical activity alone cannot establish stored content. Packing success needs retained task function at the claimed physical scale with support costs disclosed. Exit: carrier association, selective contribution, or no supported carrier. Extraction and portability remain later claims.

## EX09 Observer and adaptive support

Question: can O predict a relevant state before the outcome, and can its advice improve C? First qualify O1 status classification. For O2 early warning, lock the feature cutoff and prediction horizon; exclude future responses and post-response error flags. Compare with digital models receiving the same information. Then compare allowed interventions chosen from O or S with fixed and action-matched schedules. A prevented adverse outcome cannot be naively scored as a false prediction; use shadow or randomized comparison periods. Exit separately for predictive value and intervention benefit. This tests useful Winds from a child network without assuming semantic error awareness.

## EX10 Join and reader compatibility

Question: does a qualified partner or P_crystal contribute the intended relation when joined to C? Compare C alone, partner alone where meaningful, joined behavior, incompatible mappings and a matched substitute or replay. Freeze bridge transformation and readers. Track actual route state, latency, general responsiveness and protected tasks. A host can use an external module without learning its relation. Exit: compatible assisted use, a generic state effect, no contribution, or an unresolved route. Electronic joining comes before any separately qualified axonal-joining study.

## EX11 Independent recipient acquisition

Question: does B retain the specific relation after its teaching connection is absent? Establish A's competence and B's baseline, then compare trained living teaching with untrained, shuffled or yoked, and equally informed software-teacher conditions. Remove live teacher routes, cached events, teacher-derived normalization and shared adaptive state for B-only evaluation. B still receives ordinary test cues through its own reader. Report independently retained learning even if software teaching works equally well; reserve a living-teacher advantage claim for a demonstrated difference. This is teacher-mediated acquisition unless physical carrier transfer itself has been independently shown.

## EX12 Revision and renewal

Question: can an intended relation change while protected relations remain available, and can a new module add capability without an unacceptable burden? Separate revision, module addition and replacement into different comparisons. Report the target correction, each protected outcome, general function, resource use and new interference. No statistical change in a neighbor is not proof of preservation; use a justified noninferiority margin. Physical distance or a diffusion-length estimate does not alone decide revisability. Replacement gives a new biological identity. Reproducing a function after retraining is functional continuity, not restoration of the old physical memory.

# Joining stages and the main organoid

J0 preserves independent readiness and baselines. J1 allows shadow observation with no influence on C. J2 enables one-way advisory messages under a fixed host policy. J3 evaluates useful joined behavior while preserving individual tests. J4 adds S only after its marginal role is justified. J5 introduces a separately qualified physical route with its own fluid, electrical and cellular coupling model. J6 considers growth or renewal through the same readiness and contribution checks.

The Crystal route uses these stages rather than bypassing them. A carrier that supplies a cue, a carrier that participates continuously in computation, a teacher that changes B, and a transferred carrier that preserves content are different interface modes. Record the mode before analysis. A portable-carrier claim requires evidence that the proposed physical object, rather than a hidden software answer channel or residual teacher, accounts for the relation.

# Analysis and experimental units

The primary unit is the independently prepared biological system appropriate to randomization. Electrodes, imaging regions, cells, trials and repeated sessions are nested observations. Record source line, donor where applicable, batch and preparation. Many electrodes on one organoid do not create many independent organoids. Block randomization by known preparation factors and blind scoring to assignment where feasible.

Predeclare one primary estimand per study. Examples are the preparation-level difference in delayed macro recall between contingent and exposure-matched teaching, or the change in a protected capability after joining. Use a model or resampling scheme that respects nesting and the bounded outcome. Account for batches and repeated testing; a nominal two-sample formula does not settle the actual design. Determine sample size from justified effect and variability assumptions, attrition and the analysis model. Document assumptions rather than inventing pilot data.

Report absolute delayed scores alongside change from immediate performance. A chance-corrected retention ratio may exceed one, become negative or be unstable when the immediate advantage is near zero. Do not clamp it to manufacture an interpretable range. Do not combine macro recall with a raw-accuracy chance value. Use metric-specific nulls and report their construction. When the denominator is too small for the predefined applicability rule, omit the ratio and report the absolute estimates.

Equivalence and noninferiority answer different questions from failure to reject a difference. A nonsignificant result does not show an absent mechanism. Choose margins using scientific importance and measurement precision before confirmation. Do not inherit generic plus-or-minus 0.05 bounds for quantities with different units. Predefine multiplicity families and how primary and exploratory results will be reported. Repeated development belongs to development; a revised candidate needs new confirmation data.

Retrieval probes can themselves modify the system. Record every probe and separate repeated-probe from minimally probed designs where necessary. Exclusions concern validity and prespecified preparation criteria, not whether a result supports the theory. Report all enrolled preparations, missing outcomes, pauses, invalid trials and attrition. A healthy surviving subset may be selected by the intervention; do not silently treat it as the original randomized comparison.

# Budgets and controlled unknowns

For an illustrative stream of 1,020 channels at 20,000 samples per second with two stored bytes per sample, raw throughput is 40.8 decimal MB per second, 146.88 GB per hour and 3.52512 TB per day. These are arithmetic assumptions inherited from v0.4, not a newly confirmed product specification. Actual packing, metadata, optical data, simultaneous channels and export behavior must be verified for the purchased platform. Optical storage is budgeted separately.

Measure acquisition, processing, bridge, queue and delivery latency end to end. A mean delay alone is insufficient; report tails, jitter, missed deadlines and clock drift. An eligibility-window claim requires the relevant preparation's measured timescale. Do not prescribe a universal delay sweep from the metaphor. Energy accounting includes host, acquisition, imaging, incubation, storage, idle care, failed preparations and attributable services. Learned organization is not an energy source.

# Development sequence and completion criteria

WP0 freezes terminology and the source reconciliation. WP1 implements the task, records, leakage tests and nonbiological fault harness. WP2 resolves source and platform compatibility. WP3 qualifies the interface and support system. WP4 establishes acquisition and delayed Roots in the actual preparation. WP5 investigates one local molecular or carrier mechanism. WP6 qualifies O and useful advisory support. WP7 tests joining and independent B acquisition in separate campaigns. WP8 evaluates revision, renewal and any justified physical portability.

The immediate handoff is concrete: use the task oracle and templates to complete the analysis contract; select the preparation version and platform; close its interface and support unknowns; then attempt EX04. EX02 and EX03 can be a separate molecular track when receiver and assays are available. Neither track is called cheap or runnable next week without an actual assay inventory and budget.

A development report is complete when it includes the frozen question, all relevant records, analysis, exclusions, uncertainty, competing explanations and the resulting claim status. A Crystal claim additionally needs the bounded carrier and its content-specific contribution. A full-mind, semantic knowledge-transfer or brain-integration claim is not a consequence of passing these bounded gates. The purpose of the design is to make each next result interpretable enough to decide what should be built or tested next.
