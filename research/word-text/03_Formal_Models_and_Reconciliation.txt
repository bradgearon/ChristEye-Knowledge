Purpose and precedence
This supplement audits the supplied Living Computer Theory Set, Equations Explained v1.5, Lotus Stream
Research notes, Physical Learning Device Design v0.4, and the two Wind, Water and Crystal documents
attributed by the user to Claude. Their originals remain unchanged. This edition supplies explicit dispositions so
that an older equation is not accidentally reused with a corrected paragraph that contradicts it.
Mathematical correctness, identifiability, empirical fit and biological interpretation are separate tests. A
familiar equation can be mathematically valid and biologically inapplicable. A standard model's failure can
reveal a violated assumption or missing process; it is not necessarily experimental error. All equations below
are definitions or candidate models unless a narrower standard identity is explicitly identified. No fit or
biological parameter was estimated for this package.

Worked correction of the maintenance equation
The supplied C3 equation is:


For nonnegative s and positive s_max, it can be written as s[(α − δ) − αs/s_max]. Its equilibria are zero and
s_star = s_max(1 − δ/α), when the latter is positive. The derivative at zero is α − δ. The derivative at s_star is −
(α − δ).
When α exceeds δ, zero is unstable and the positive equilibrium is stable. Every sufficiently small positive state
grows toward that equilibrium in this deterministic model. There is no positive unstable threshold below which
the state must decay to zero. When α is below δ, every positive state decays; at equality the nonlinear term
still drives positive states toward zero. The claimed bistability and separatrix therefore do not follow.

Word processing and receiver context
A reaction rate depends on the identified substrate, enzyme state, compartment and conditions. The
competing-enzyme fraction formula in P1 can be a useful approximation for a shared, low-substrate pool with
suitable kinetics and negligible competing processes. It is not automatically applicable to intracellular furin
processing, extracellular cleavage and astrocytic uptake and recycling together. Product interconversion and
degradation also affect the measured fractions.
The relevant molecular question is broader than termini. A receiver can distinguish availability, location,
multimeric state, binding partners and sequence of exposure. Conversely, a measured chemical difference may
have no task-relevant effect. The reported plasmin/furin boundary distinction motivates form identification; it
does not establish a universal MMP-9-specific content code. [S03]
For a fractional effect with an appropriate independence interpretation, Bliss uses E_AB = E_A + E_B − E_A E_B.
Loewe instead asks about dose equivalence under its applicable response assumptions. Neither is simply the
sum of two arbitrary readouts. Scaling any receptor signal to zero and one does not by itself justify
independence. Report the model, endpoint, uncertainty and sensitivity to reasonable alternatives. [S22]
Order sensitivity can arise from receptor preparation, adaptation, exposure duration, trafficking or residual
compound. Demonstrating a proposed preparatory Word requires an observed intermediate state and
evidence connecting that state to the later response. A null order difference can reflect rapid preparation
during simultaneous exposure; it does not collapse every molecular Word into one. The number of comparison
conditions must be counted explicitly: two orders, simultaneous, each-alone and vehicle conditions are not
simply four arms.

For the original plasticity model with decay rate λ, define Δw relative to baseline. Its exact linear solution is the
decayed initial Δw plus η times the integral of m(u)q(u)e(u) weighted by exp[−λ(t−u)]. Replacing the integrand
with the product of separate time averages needs assumptions about constancy or covariance. The claimed
saturation value is an equilibrium under constant drive; it is not a universal training plateau.
For observed performance P = P0 + (K − P0)A, both K and A are latent without extra measurements. Recovered

Statistics and dimensional checks
For an idealized independent two-arm normal comparison with known common standard deviation σ and
target difference Δ, a planning approximation is n per arm approximately 2(z_alpha + z_power)²σ²/Δ². It is not
the final design for clustered cultures, multiple contrasts, bounded scores or noninferiority. A coefficient of
variation is σ divided by the mean; a CV of 15 percent does not supply σ without that mean and scale.
Technical wells cannot substitute for independent biological units.
Report target change and collateral change separately before calculating a locality ratio. When the target
change is close to zero, division can produce unstable or misleading values. When comparing a diffusion length
sqrt(D/k) with a site spacing, D must have length-squared per time units and k inverse-time units. A single
decay length does not define a hard edge: thresholds, binding, geometry, localization and measured exposure
matter.
Electrical charge is the integral of current over time. Electrical energy is the integral of voltage times current
over time. For a known constant resistance, current squared times resistance can recover energy; the neural
interface is not assumed to be such a fixed resistor. Pulse counts, current, charge, impedance and energy are
different records. Matching one does not imply the others are matched.


Model selection and next use
Start with a small model per question: a task-performance comparison for Roots, a defined receiver-state
model for order sensitivity, and a carrier-specific accounting model for packing. Test identifiability and
measurement adequacy before estimating parameters. Use discovery data to choose plausible forms and
reserve confirmation for the declared contrasts. Avoid a single large equation in which enough adjustable rates
can explain every outcome.
The useful contribution of the formal work is the set of alternatives it makes distinguishable. If quiet does not
help, remove that requirement. If a matrix timing effect does not appear, narrow that mechanism. If no
physical carrier can be linked to content, retain the learning result without calling it a Crystal. If a carrier works
only while joined, report compatible assisted use. These are informative outcomes that keep the plan
connected to observable behavior.
