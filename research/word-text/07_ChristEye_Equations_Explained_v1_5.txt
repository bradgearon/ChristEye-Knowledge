1. Network Dynamics
x_{t+1} = f(W x_t + B c_t + G a_t + D z_t)
Population firing-rate (or binned spike-count) vector at the next time step. W is recurrent connectivity, c is
the encoded stimulus, a is astrocytic state, z is culture environment (Soil). f is a nonlinear activation.
Science: Direct descendant of the Wilson–Cowan equations (Wilson & Cowan, Biophys J 1972; Kybernetik
1973), the standard mean-field model of interacting excitatory and inhibitory populations. Widely used to
interpret multi-electrode array recordings.

2. Astrocytic State
τ_a ⊙ da/dt = −a + F(x, z, a)
Slow astrocyte variable driven by neuronal activity and environment. Used as a candidate additional feature
for retention prediction.
Science: Reduced calcium or activity models are common. Recent in-vivo evidence (Williamson et al.,
Nature 2024/2025) shows sparse learning-associated astrocyte ensembles in hippocampus are necessary
and sufficient for certain memory-recall forms and can induce LTP-like changes in engram neurons.
Culture-level transfer remains untested.

3. Eligibility Trace and Milk × Honey Plasticity
τ_e de_ij/dt = −e_ij + Φ(x_i, x_j, Δt)
dw_ij/dt = η m(t) q(t) e_ij − λ (w_ij − w_ij^0)
Local spike-timing eligibility trace is multiplied by both contingent feedback m (Honey) and support
adequacy q (Milk) before a weight changes. Decay returns weights toward baseline.
Science: Classic STDP is established (Markram et al. 1997; Bi & Poo 1998). Reward- or dopamine-
modulated STDP acting on eligibility traces is documented in vivo and slice. The product form makes both
requirements explicit and independently measurable.

4. Milk as Bounded Range
q(t) = ∏ exp[−½ (max(0, l_k − z_k, z_k − u_k)/s_k)^2]
q equals 1 only while every culture variable stays inside its locked physiological band and falls smoothly
outside. Excess never raises q above 1.
Science: Standard culture qualification practice. Bands come from platform and media validation, not free
parameters.

5. Roots × Access
P_obs = P_chance + (K − P_chance) A , 0 ≤ A ≤ 1
Observed performance is retained capacity gated by momentary access. A failed trial does not prove
capacity is lost. Discriminating test: restore conditions with feedback off; recovery without new training
means access was low.
Science: Standard distinction in behavioral neuroscience and closed-loop experiments between storage
and retrieval.

6. Training Encode / Decode and DishBrain-style Loop
r_k = r_min + (r_max − r_min) s_k
ŷ = argmax_c (spike sum in region); confidence = softmax
Feature vector is rate-coded onto input sites; decision is the most active output region after artifact masking.
Contingent structured feedback is delivered.
Science: Identical in structure to the DishBrain closed-loop system (Kagan et al., Neuron 2022). Cultures
showed performance gains under contingent feedback absent in stimulus-only and non-neural controls.

7–12. Remaining Equations
Learning metrics (LI, d′, ECE), retention R_U, transfer G, flexibility F, ΔL astroengram information test,
electronic bridge, teaching effect T, Lotus J with UNRESOLVED outcome, Heart hard veto, data rate 40.8
MB/s, sample-size formula, residual mutual information Mesh edges, Water flooding, access-failure, novelty
residual, and attunement-prison flags follow the same pattern: each is a standard or cleanly derived
measurable form. Supporting science is ordinary signal-detection theory, closed-loop interfacing, decision
theory under uncertainty, and network-inference methods. Hypothesis-labeled items (astrocyte contribution
causality, living-teacher superiority) remain experiments, not findings.
All equations remain research models. E-labeled items rest on established methods; H-labeled items
require the experiments defined in the gates. Negative results are informative. Nonclinical only.
